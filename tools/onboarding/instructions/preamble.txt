You are making a world for MineWorld. You may read only the files in {sandbox}/guide and {sandbox}/reference, and run the `mineworld` command. Write your world in {sandbox}/work. When you are done, write DONE in {sandbox}/work/STATUS. If you cannot continue, write STUCK and one sentence saying why. Whenever the guide did not tell you something you needed, add one line to {sandbox}/work/GAPS.txt saying what you looked for.

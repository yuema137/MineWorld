## ARC-141 — The S26 gates restated per assembled outfit, the wardrobe gates W-1…W-12 and the no-stock scan V-1

**Date** 2026-10-19 · **Status** accepted (instruments in S32-p; bounds unchanged from step-32 §7) · **Approved by** the freeze of `pr-s32p-wardrobe-pilot.md` (2026-10-15) · **Relates to** `ARC-23`, `ARC-138`, `ARC-140`, `DEP-72` · **Design** `step-32-wardrobe.md` §§7.1–7.3; `pr-s32p-wardrobe-pilot.md` §4.8; `tools/character_pipeline/PIPELINE.md` §17

**Problem.** #211's gates judge one fused mesh. A modular character is a body plus parts, and a part can fail in ways a fused mesh cannot (poke-through, a wrong hidden set, a stock asset slipped in).

**Choice.** G-1…G-14 are judged on the assembled outfit (G-1 counts body plus parts; G-2 one material per part, `MW_Cast` for the body and `MW_Cast_<id>` per part; G-5 every part's skin is the body's; G-14 provenance on every part). W-1…W-12 are step-32 §7.2's, with the pilot's instruments: W-1 in `garment_format.py`; W-2, W-3, W-12 and V-1 in `wgates.py`; W-4 on every pose-sheet frame in `pose_sheet.gd` (D-impl-10); W-9 in `cast.py wardrobe w9`. V-1 refuses a drawn part whose provenance names a stock MPFB2/MakeHuman asset, a fixture or the fitting template, or names no pilot Meshy task and no pilot tight-fit body. Every bound is a literal with its source (I-p-6). The review page withholds the frames of an outfit whose V-1 is not PASS.

**Consequences.** A modular character is judged by the same tier bar as the fused Vera, plus the failure modes a wardrobe adds. The pilot's results per outfit are on `VIS-3D-WARDROBE-P` and in `pilot/s32p/report.json`.

**Revisit** when S32-c moves fitting into the client (W-9 becomes the client's check).

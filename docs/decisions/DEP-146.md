## DEP-146 — Adopted: the 100STYLE `Neutral` idle (CC BY 4.0) as the standing loop of every body

**Date** 2026-10-19 · **Status** accepted · **Approved by** operator, primary session, 2026-10-19 (approval of
`mineworld-demos/research/natural-pose-tools.md`; PR S26-c-pose design freeze) · **Relates to** `DEP-8`, `ARC-93`,
[`REUSE_POLICY.md`](../REUSE_POLICY.md) · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-s26c-natural-pose.md` part C

**Problem.** The standing pose was procedural (`human.gd` `stand_key`) and, per body, searched (`stance.gd`). The
operator rejected both: hands reached forward and some palms faced outward. A real person's relaxed stand
(weight shifts, slight elbow flexion, forearms in their resting mid-rotation) should come from a recording.

**Options read at source (2026-10-19).**
- Quaternius UAL 1 Standard (CC0): its one idle, `Idle_Loop`, is an action-game idle, already rejected.
- Quaternius UAL 1 Pro/Source and UAL 2 (CC0): paid, or no published idle list.
- CMU mocap: licence wording ambiguous, and unverifiable here.
- HDM05: CC BY-SA, whose ShareAlike cannot sit in the MIT tree.
- Bandai Namco and LaFAN1: non-commercial.
- Mixamo: no redistribution (`DEP-8`).
- SMPL/AMASS family: non-commercial.
- **100STYLE** (Mason, Starke & Komura 2022): the project page states "This work is licensed under a Creative
  Commons Attribution 4.0 International License"; every style has an idle take, one style is `Neutral`.

**Choice.** Adopt 100STYLE `Neutral_ID.bvh` (SHA-256 `71aead2e…f259f`, downloaded 2026-10-19 from the page's own
link).
- **Conversion.** `clients/3d-spike/tools/blender/stand_from_bvh.py` converts it offline: Blender's BVH importer; the
  standing part looped; the fourth chest joint folded into its neighbours in world space; renamed to the client's
  bone map; the root's drift removed.
- **Result.** It is committed as `assets/characters/stand_lib.glb`. It imports through `vitruvian_bonemap.tres` +
  `SkeletonProfileHumanoid` + the rest fixer, the same path as every body and the UAL clips (no new dependency).
- **Attribution.** CC BY 4.0 permits adaptation and redistribution with attribution. The attribution, the licence and
  the list of changes travel with the clip (`assets/characters/LICENSE.100style.txt`, `clients/3d-spike/ASSETS.md`).
- **`DEP-8`.** The operator rules that CC BY with attribution passes its relicensing test for presentation assets.
- **Exclusion corrected.** `ASSETS.md`'s earlier "100STYLE excluded on licence" line is corrected. SMPL-fitted
  copies of 100STYLE inside AMASS-style redistributions remain excluded.
- **Fallback.** Not used: the Neutral idle is relaxed and clean on all 22 bodies. The fallback would have been a
  Quaternius CC0 idle.

**Consequences.**
- The clip has no finger data; finger curl stays as the bodies' rest.
- One performer's proportions reach 22 bodies through the standard retarget; the stand gates (PIPELINE §15) check
  the result on every body and every frame.
- A World or Presentation Pack may ship its own idle set through the same bone-map path.

**Revisit** if the dataset's licence changes, or a CC0 relaxed idle on our rig becomes available.

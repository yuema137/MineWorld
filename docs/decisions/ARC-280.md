## ARC-280 — Punctual parts of a day, and the next part disclosed with the agenda

**Date** 2026-10-18 · **Status** accepted (operator, 2026-10-18: PR LE revision 2 frozen; Q-LE-2, Q-LE-7 as recommended) · **Approved by** the operator, in the primary session · **Relates to** `ARC-32` (schedule moves nobody), `ARC-90`; supersedes ruling R-TWc-FX24 (a) (Q-LE-1) · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-le-leave-early.md` SD-LE-1, SD-LE-2, §0.3

**Problem.** A routine part names one instant, `from`, and schedule uses it as the moment the agenda changes. A person whose day says "at work from 05:30" therefore set out at 05:30 and arrived one walk late, whatever drove them: in Market Town no shift had ever begun with its employee present (120 of 120 measured, `pr-le-leave-early.md` §2.1). And schedule disclosed only the part in force, so no controller could set out before the boundary even if it wanted to.

**Options considered.**

| Option | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| (a) **A `punctual` flag on the part being entered** | **Chosen**: whether a boundary is about arriving or leaving is a property of the part |
| (b) Every part arrival-anchored | Declined: Alice would leave her shift early for the park |
| (c) A separate `arrive:` time per part | Declined: a second clock time the author must keep consistent with `from` |
| (d) Move each boundary earlier in content (R-TWc-FX24 (a)) | Superseded: right for one host's stride cadence only |

**Choice.** `Segment` gains `punctual: bool`, `#[serde(default, skip_serializing_if = "is_false")]`, documented in the authored schema: *the person means to be at the place when this part begins, so whoever drives them sets out early enough to arrive by `from`.* Schedule carries the flag and never interprets it: it states the same two facts, its Process wakes at the same boundaries, and an unflagged routine keeps its payload bytes. `ScheduleSystem::discloses` builds the `agenda` record as `DisclosedAgenda`: the stored `Agenda`'s fields plus `next { place, label, from, punctual }`, the part in force at the agenda's `until` (the midnight wrap and "last part → first part tomorrow" are `Segments::at`'s). The component type stays `agenda`; the stored component, its schema version and every fact are unchanged; it is disclosed to its holder only (`INV-13`). The Routine as a whole stays undisclosed: one part of lookahead is what a person keeps in mind.

**Consequences.** A world that marks no part punctual states the same facts and stores the same bytes (I-LE-1, I-LE-5). Every reader of the `agenda` record still decodes it (`Agenda` has no `deny_unknown_fields`). The flag is YAML-boolean like every other flag the loader reads: `yes` and a quoted `"true"` are booleans under serde-saphyr's default; a number or another word is refused at its position. Content marks Alice's 05:30 work and Felix's 08:00 store part punctual, identically in Social Café and Market Town (`ARC-35` check 3).

**Revisit** if an author needs a lead time per part rather than per person or per controller; that would be content data on the part, decided separately.

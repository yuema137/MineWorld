## ARC-182 — Controllers read the market packs by slug through wire mirrors that an owner-side test holds equal

**Date** 2026-10-26 · **Status** accepted · **Approved by** the operator, 2026-10-20, primary session (Q-S31d-1 ruled (a)) · **Relates to** `ARC-35` (check 2 unchanged; item 6 extended), `ARC-34`, `ARC-117` · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-s31d-reporter-policies.md` §5.7

**Problem.** The pricing policies (`ARC-117`) live in `rule-controller`, a framework crate under `ARC-35` check 2: no normal path may lead from it to a market pack, and no file under `cognition/` may name a market crate. Yet a policy must read economy's `asks`, `shop` and `currency` records, inventory's `holdings`, item's `item-catalogue`, and submit economy's `set-price` and `list-for-sale`.

**Options considered.** (a) **Read the records and build the requests by slug, through small mirror types with the owner's labels, held equal to the owners' types by a test on the owner's side** — chosen. (b) Amend check 2 so that cognition crates may depend on the market packs — rejected: the market would no longer be removable from a controller's build.

**Choice.**

1. `mineworld_rule_controller::price::wire` defines `AsksSeen`, `ListingSeen`, `CurrencySeen`, `HoldingsSeen`, `CatalogueSeen` (each a `Component` with the owner's component type, owner system id and schema version), `SetPrice` and `ListForSale` (each an `Action` with the owner's action type and owner), and `BuySeen` (decode only). Every mirror refuses unknown fields; `payload_for` refuses a newer schema, so an owner that bumps its schema makes the policy do nothing rather than guess.
2. `systems/economy/tests/controller_wire.rs` — where both sides may be named — builds each real record with its owner's code in hand-built worlds, decodes it with the mirror, compares field by field and schema version by version, and decodes each mirror request with economy's own types. A renamed field or a bumped schema turns it red.
3. A world without the market still builds the controller and decides exactly as before: the records are absent and the offers are not made.

**Consequences.** The market stays removable and check 2 is unchanged. Each owner change to a mirrored shape updates `price::wire` and the guard in the same PR. S31-h's haggling requests are built through three more mirrors. `rule-controller`'s fetch band (S35 N-h) already read `holdings`, `shop-stock` and `buy` by slug with local decode structs; the mirrors are the same idea with the owner's labels and a guard.

## ARC-140 — The runtime garment format: a neutral-rest mesh plus a `.mhclo`-style binding to the `hm08` template, fitted at load (format part)

**Date** 2026-10-19 · **Status** accepted for the pilot's format; the runtime adoption waits on `ARC-138` · **Approved by** the freeze of `pr-s32p-wardrobe-pilot.md` (2026-10-15) · **Relates to** `ARC-138`, `DEP-72`, `ARC-141` · **Design** `step-32-wardrobe.md` §§6.2–6.3; `pr-s32p-wardrobe-pilot.md` §4.4; `tools/character_pipeline/mwcast/wardrobe/garment_format.py`

**Problem.** One garment must fit every body without a per-body copy, and the client must place it without MPFB2.

**Choice.** A garment folder holds `garment.yaml`, `garment.glb` (one surface, no skin), `binding.bin`, `hides.bin` and textures; each body carries `<subject>_template.bin`, `_template_weights.bin` and `_corr.bin`. The layouts are `garment_format.py`'s docstring: `binding.bin` (`MWGB`, version 1, three scale references, then per garment vertex three template vertex ids, three weights and an offset); `hides.bin` (`MWGH`, sorted template vertex ids); `corr.bin` (`MWGC`, per body vertex three template vertex ids and two barycentrics, D-impl-5); `template.bin` (`MWGT`, 19,158 positions); `template_weights.bin` (`MWGW`, its joint table rewritten into the body's glTF skin order, D-impl-6). A fitted vertex is `Σ wᵢ·T[vᵢ] + offset·(|T[a]−T[b]| / rest)` per axis, MakeHuman's formula; the same formula runs in Python (`garment_format.fit`), Blender (`assemble.py`) and GDScript (`fit_at_load.gd`), and W-9 holds them within 0.5 mm. Each part reuses the body's `Skin` (D-p-2). Pilot amendment (D-impl-12): a garment is bound against the template fitted to the clay figure it was generated on, not against its wearer's `T_P`.

**Consequences.** The client needs no MPFB2 code; a garment costs one mesh and ≈ 40 bytes per vertex of binding. The format is versioned by its magic and version field.

**Revisit** with S32-c's client integration or a route-B rejection.

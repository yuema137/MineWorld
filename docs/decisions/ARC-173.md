## ARC-173 — A client draws an activity only when the world discloses it; an undisclosed Process is not drawn

**Date** 2026-10-20 · **Status** proposed in PR S33-g ("Lakeside's own people in 3D"); pending the operator's
merge review · **Relates to** `ARC-91`, `ARC-92`, `ARC-66`, step-33 I-S33-5 and F-S33-6,
`.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-s33g-people.md` §§3.2 (F-S33g-1), 5.4, 14 (Q-S33g-1, ruled 2026-10-20)

**Problem.** A living world invites its clients to draw what people are doing: a fisherman with a rod at
the water's edge, a rower on the lake, a baker at the oven. The authoritative state of such an activity is
a `Process` owned by a System Pack, and an `Observation` has no Process field
(`contracts/src/observation.rs`): another person's activity is visible to an observer only through a
component or an event its owning pack discloses. The fishing pack (`mineworld-pack-fishing`, pinned under
`ARC-66`) tells only the angler (`fishing-started`) and discloses its `Angler` component to nobody else. A
client that drew someone fishing because they stand still at the water, or because a routine says
"05:00 pier fishing", would draw a behaviour the world never stated, and would draw it differently from
another client that guesses differently.

**Options considered.** (A) Draw an activity only from a disclosure that names it; ask the owning pack to
disclose what others may see (chosen, Q-S33g-1 as recommended). (B) Accept that nobody else is ever seen
doing anything (folds into A: it is A while no pack discloses). (C) Infer the activity from position,
stillness, a routine or a tag (rejected: the client would be deciding what the world is, `CLAUDE.md` §4
item 15, and two clients would disagree).

**Decision.**
1. A client draws a person doing an activity -- fishing, rowing, baking at the oven, any other -- only
   from a disclosed component or event that names that activity, and only while that disclosure holds.
   An undisclosed Process is not drawn, for any pack.
2. Locomotion (standing, walking, jogging, drawn from observed positions) and sitting (drawn only where
   the world seats the person) need no activity disclosure; they are not activities in this sense.
3. The player's own client may draw its own player doing an activity from the player's own observation
   (for fishing: its own `fishing-started` while its `catch` runs), because that is a disclosure to that
   observer.
4. Decorative figures (presentation people with no world entity) never perform an activity and never
   sit: no disclosure can name them (S33-g F-S33g-3, F-S33g-4).
5. In the 3D client the rule is held by `SliceActivities` (`clients/3d-spike/scripts/slice/activities.gd`):
   a figure's animation tree may play only the unconditioned clips of item 2 while no disclosure names an
   activity, and the connected route probe (`./mineworld-slice --world=<key> --link`) fails by name for
   any figure or decor figure whose body could play another clip.
6. Fishing in Lakeside (Q-S33g-1, operator 2026-10-20): nobody but the angler is drawn fishing until the
   fishing pack discloses its `Angler` to persons present; that release is the pack owner's, re-pinned in
   its own MineWorld PR under `ARC-66`.

**Consequences.** Lakeside's people are seen standing on the pier and the shore, not fishing, until the
fishing pack discloses. A pack that wants its activity seen discloses it; no client grows a guess. A new
activity clip in the 3D client fails the probe until the code that plays it is tied to a disclosure.

**Revisit** when a pack discloses an activity to persons present, to add the clip under that disclosure.

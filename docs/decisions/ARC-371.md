## ARC-371 — A world states the time of day it begins (`world.yaml` `begins:`)

**Date** 2026-11-27 · **Status** accepted — outcome ruled by the operator 2026-11-27 ("新世界早上 7 点开始"); mechanism ratified by the operator 2026-11-27 ("begins 字段按设计确认"), both relayed by the primary
coordinator; implemented in PR #564 · **Relates to** `ARC-25`, `ARC-61` item 7, `ARC-69`
item 9, `ARC-364`, `ARC-370`, `INV-TW-4` · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-world-begins.md`

**Problem.** Every host began every world at instant 0, local midnight (`INV-TW-4`). With shop hours
(`ARC-364`) a newly hosted Market Town opened with every shop closed for hours. The operator ruled that new
worlds begin at 07:00.

**Choice.**
1. `world.yaml` may state `begins: "HH:MM"`: the time of day of genesis on day 0. Absent means `"00:00"`, the
   behaviour before this decision. Owned by `worldpack`, read with the manifest, refused at load when not a
   strict `HH:MM`, naming the file and key.
2. Every host that creates a world — `run` (in memory or a new save) and `server` (ephemeral or a new save) —
   begins it at that instant. A resumed save keeps the genesis instant it was created with, and the server's
   paced consult origin is that instant; `begins` is not a configuration fact, so changing it is not drift.
3. Midnights stay multiples of 86 400 s; genesis need not be one. No kernel, System Pack or client changes.
4. Market Town, Social Café and Lakeside begin at 07:00; Bodies Yard is unchanged.

**Not in this decision.** Starting on another day of the calendar, per-host overrides, changing an existing save's
start.

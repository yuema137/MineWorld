## ARC-138 — Route B: a modular character is a Meshy base-layer body, a hidden `hm08` fitting template registered to it, and separately generated parts

**Date** 2026-10-19 · **Status** **rejected as built** (operator ruling on `VIS-3D-WARDROBE-P`, 2026-10-19, relayed by the coordinator); levers **L-a** (a chroma-key clay figure for garment references) and **L-b** (push garments out of the body at assembly) chosen, capped at 540 Meshy credits and $1.5 of image calls for L-a; the full-cast wardrobe (≈ 3,330 credits) is not approved and will be asked once L-a passes on Vera, Ivan and Hana · **Approved by** the operator's ruling Q-S32-1 (2026-10-15: route B, "v2 + Meshy 分件生成，可换装") and the freeze of `pr-s32p-wardrobe-pilot.md` ("同意冻结并实施") · **Relates to** `DEP-72`, `DEP-73`, `DEP-74`, `ARC-140`, `ARC-141`, `ARC-94` · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/step-32-wardrobe.md` §§5.2, 6.1; `pr-s32p-wardrobe-pilot.md` §§4.1–4.3, 16

**Problem.** A fused Meshy character (Vera) cannot change clothes: body, garments and hair are one mesh with one texture. A wardrobe needs a body that can be dressed, and garments that fit every body.

**Choice (route B, as the pilot built it).** Per person: (1) a template-v2 image of the person in a grey tank top and shorts with a skin-tone cap (R-1, operator-picked); (2) one Meshy image-to-3D run of it (R-2); (3) #211's stages 6–10 at 60,000 triangles with the 52-joint rig (R-3); (4) MPFB2's `hm08` template registered onto that body by joint pose, linear-blend skinning and a surface fit, with weights from the body (R-4). Parts are generated once on their first wearer (R-6…R-11) and fitted to any body through the template (`ARC-140`).

**What the pilot measured (2026-10-19).** The chain runs end to end on Vera, Ivan and Hana for 30 credits per body and 30 per garment (the ledger, §16). The look does not reach the fused Vera: the subtraction of the clay mannequin from a garment download loses large parts of low-contrast garments (the mid-grey jeans, the sage jacket), keeps clay where the template misses (hands), and the garments fitted onto the body poke through it in every pose-sheet frame (W-4). Registration on a Meshy body is 1–8 mm mean per region on Vera and Hana, and 24–73 mm on Ivan's torso, face and upper arms (his rig failed G-6). The pilot therefore reports route B **not adopted as built**; the levers are on the review page and in the ledger.

**Consequences.** S32-c, S32-d and S32-e wait on the verdict. If the operator rejects route B, this record moves to `rejected` with the levers that were tried; if the operator accepts a lever, a follow-up pilot is a new PR.

**Ruling (2026-10-19).** Route B is rejected as built. The follow-up applies L-a and L-b to the same three people
and is judged on the same page (before/after). Spend: L-a ≤ 540 Meshy credits and ≤ $1.5 OpenAI; ask before
exceeding.

**Revisit** when L-a + L-b have been judged on Vera, Ivan and Hana.

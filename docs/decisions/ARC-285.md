## ARC-285 — `nourishment:` is a section the `needs` pack owns on item files

**Date** 2026-10-18 · **Status** accepted (design, step 35); to be implemented by PR N-b · **Approved by** the operator, in the primary session, 2026-10-18 (step 35 frozen; the generic-mechanism ruling below) · **Relates to** `ARC-31`, `ARC-36`, `ARC-37`, `ARC-284`, `MODULE_SPEC.md` §4.1 (sections) · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/step-35-needs-and-sleep.md` §§5.1, 5.3, 5.5, 7

**Problem.** Eating soup should restore more hunger than eating an apple, and a cup of coffee none. The amount belongs with the item kind, because an author writes it once per kind. But the `item` pack owns the item file's `item:` section, and it must not learn what hunger is. And `consumption`, which offers `eat`, owns no state and is "the interaction, not a need".

**Options considered.**

| Option | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| (a) A field in `item:` (`item: { category: food, hunger: 600 }`) | Declined: `item` would learn about needs, and removing `needs` would leave a field nobody reads |
| (b) Restore by category (`food` restores a fixed amount) | Declined: step-24 §6.5 started there, but soup and an apple would nourish alike, and a world could not tune one kind |
| (c) A table of amounts in `configure/needs.yaml`, keyed by item | Declined: two places to keep in step when an author adds a kind; an Entity Pack's kinds could not carry their own amounts |
| (d) **A section `nourishment:` on item files, owned by `needs`** (`ARC-31`, extended to items by `ARC-36`) | **Chosen** |

**Choice.** `nourishment: { hunger: <0 … 1 000> }` on an item file that declares a kind. The `needs` pack declares and decodes it, and `needs` alone reads it, when it reduces inventory's `items-consumed`: hunger falls by `nourishment.hunger` × count. An item kind without the section nourishes 0. The section is disclosed to nobody. It is refused at load, by name and with line and column, when the world does not enable `needs` (`ARC-31` rule 6), when the world does not enable `consumption` (nothing could ever eat the item, `MODULE_SPEC.md` §4.1), when a value is out of bound (`hunger: 1 001`), and when a key is unknown.

**The generic-mechanism ruling (operator, 2026-10-18).** No item is named in code. Market Town's menu values (soup 600, sandwich 650, bread 500, cake 400, croissant 350, apple 200, juice 100, milk 150, coffee 0, tea 0; step 35 §5.5, authored) are content in N-d's item files, and a second world sets its own.

**Consequences.** An Entity Pack's item files may carry `nourishment:`, decoded with the requiring world's enabled systems like any section (`ARC-71`). Removing `needs` means removing its sections too, or the world is refused by name rather than silently keeping inert data. The section shape is open to a later need (`thirst`) as a new optional key, with its own bound.

**Revisit** when a second need is restored by eating or drinking, or when an item's effect depends on more than its kind.

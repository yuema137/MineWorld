## DEP-140 — Needs and sleep: methods and data reused, no needs library adopted

**Date** 2026-10-18 · **Status** accepted (design, step 35); no dependency added · **Approved by** the operator, in the primary session, 2026-10-18 (step 35 frozen, its §14 DEP-N-a as recommended) · **Relates to** `CLAUDE.md` §4 rule 16, `REUSE_POLICY.md` §§11–12 (a record both when adopting and when declining), `DEP-47`, `DEP-48`, `ARC-86`, `ARC-283`, `ARC-284`, `ARC-286` · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/step-35-needs-and-sleep.md` §§2.1, 5.5, 6.3, 14, 16

**Problem.** Step 35 adds needs (hunger, tiredness) and sleep. `REUSE_POLICY.md` asks both halves of the reuse question: are we reinventing a mature wheel, and are we forcing a wheel where it does not fit?

**Options considered.**

| Candidate | Verdict and reason |
| --- | --- |
| The Sims' motives (decay over time, restored by using objects) and Dave Mark's utility curves | **Adopted as method**, already recorded in `DEP-47`: needs as decaying levels, and `motivated`'s new considerations as integer response curves |
| BLS ATUS A-3 2025 (*Sleeping*, *Eating and drinking* by clock hour) | **Reused as data**, already committed under `DEP-48` (`cognition/motivated/data/atus-a3-2025.tsv` with its `NOTICE`). It sets the default rates, the night window and the rhythm bounds of AC-S35-8 |
| The AASM/SRS consensus on adult sleep (Watson et al., *Sleep* 38(6), 2015) | **Reused as a cited reference** for "at least 7 h"; secondary in the design session, re-read at N-b's freeze before its numbers are relied on |
| The lazy-decay pattern of FG-a (`ARC-86`) | **Reused** from our own code: stored `(value, at)`, the current value computed where read, no Process |
| A utility-AI or needs crate (for example `big-brain`, declined in `DEP-47`) | **Declined.** The step-35 design session found none that runs inside a replayed, integer-only, server-authoritative simulation without bringing its own execution model (`CLAUDE.md` §4 rule 16); the needs arithmetic is a clamp and a product, a few dozen lines |

**Choice.** No dependency is added. `needs` and `sleep` are our own System Packs over the kernel's contracts. The method, the data and the lazy pattern are reused as listed.

**Consequences.** The defaults cite their sources in step 35 §5.5 and §16, and the numbers are fixed before any run (`ARC-23`). No new licence enters the repository.

**Revisit** if a maintained, engine-independent, deterministic needs or utility library appears, or when a third restorer or need makes a catalog worth comparing against an existing one.

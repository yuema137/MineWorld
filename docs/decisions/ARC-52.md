## ARC-52 — Places come from disclosure in 3D: only disclosed walls stop the player, and art only decorates

**Date** 2026-10-20 · **Status** accepted (PR 16f design frozen 2026-10-20); implemented by S14 PR 16f-b · **Approved by** the operator's freeze of `pr-16f-presentation-pack-3d.md` (2026-10-20; Q-16f-1 (A), Q-16f-2 (B), Q-16f-5, Q-16f-7 as recommended) · **Relates to** `ARC-45` (frames glued at passages; the layout is presentation), `ARC-51` (the pack the 3D client is given), `ARC-144` (the depiction seam), `ARC-146` (amended here), `ARC-307` (furnishings from data), `overall.md` §5 "Physics list" decision 3 (QPL-17) · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-16f-presentation-pack-3d.md` §§4.2-4.3

**Problem.** Before this record the 3D client's art blocked the player. Art colliders sat on the scene layer the body collides with, beside the invisible walls built from the server's `place-shape`, so the body stopped at whichever came first, and a drawn wall the server does not have could stop it. A place no depiction drew was not drawn at all, though its floor, solids and doorways were disclosed. A world with no depiction script could not be opened in 3D: bodies-yard was refused `PRESENTATION_WORLD_MISSING`. "Walls, doors and places come from disclosed geometry, and art only decorates" (QPL-17) did not hold.

**Options considered.**

| Option | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| (a) Keep art blocking beside the disclosed walls | Declined: a wall the server does not have can stop the player |
| (b) Remove art colliders | Declined: the camera would pass through drawn walls, and nothing would hold art to the disclosure |
| (c) **Art collides on its own layer, which the player's body ignores wherever the current place discloses its walls; the camera, the targeting ray and the geometry probe keep meeting it. A place no depiction draws is glued at its passage and drawn plainly from its disclosure; a world with no depiction is drawn that way entirely** | **Chosen** |

**Choice.**

1. **Blocking.** `Build.LAYER_ART` (16) carries every art collider: walls, façades, jambs, counters, lamps, trees, blockers. Floors, ramps and terrain stay on `Build.LAYER_WORLD`: the server's plan has no height, so the drawn ground is what the feet stand on. Where the current place discloses `place-shape`, the player's body masks `LAYER_WORLD | LAYER_DISCLOSED | LAYER_BODIES | LAYER_OBJECTS` and not `LAYER_ART` (`Player.set_art_blocks`), so only the server's walls stop it. Where nothing is disclosed (offline, a world without `bodies`), art still blocks, because nothing else would. The camera boom, the targeting ray and the geometry probe (`--geometry`, which now measures `LAYER_WORLD | LAYER_ART` against the disclosure) keep meeting art.
2. **One span function.** Every wall built from disclosure — the disclosed colliders, the plain shells, the stub boxes — is split at its doorways by `SliceSpans` (`clients/3d-spike/scripts/places/spans.gd`); the duplicate split is gone.
3. **Places from disclosure.** A disclosed doorway that lands on no drawn door binds its place as a **shell** (`SliceLayout.SHELL`): glued by translation at its passage (`ARC-45` point 1), never a reported miss. `SliceShells` draws a shell place from its own `place-shape` once it is stood in: a floor slab (ground, `LAYER_WORLD`), four walls outside the floor as thick as the disclosed colliders with an opening and a lintel at each doorway, and a box per shell solid. It draws only; the colliders stay `SliceClientBodies`'. The floor is the place's scene volume. A shell place one doorway away that has not been stood in gets a threshold volume just past the doorway's gap, so walking through reports the body there. A shell's volume never takes a point from the current place's volume, so the place changes only when the body leaves the current floor, through a doorway. Every place drawn stays drawn for the world instance and is dropped when the instance changes (`ARC-45` point 2).
4. **The look of a shell is the pack's.** `places/shells.yaml` (optional; YAML's JSON subset, `DEP-16`): `materials` by slug (a colour, optionally a texture of the client's library), a `default` style (`wall`, `floor`, `opening`, `solid`), and styles by tag with `.`-prefix fallback; `"wall": null` draws no walls. A place's style is the default changed by the first of its disclosed tags, in disclosed order, the file binds. A missing file draws the client's plain grey. `mineworld packs validate` refuses a faulty file `SHELLS_MALFORMED`, `SHELLS_OUT_OF_RANGE` or `SHELLS_REFERENCE`. An opening's drawn width is a drawing size, never a rule.
5. **A world with no depiction is drawn plainly** (Q-16f-1 (A)). When no depiction script and no `3d-<key>.pck` exists, `SliceWorlds.open` returns `SlicePlainDepiction`: the world's far field from the pack and plain ground, no entries. The layout anchors the first place the body stands in at the scene's origin as a shell, and every other place follows by gluing. The HUD says so (`hud.depiction.plain`). A pack without the world's far field is refused by the far-field loader (`FAR_FIELD_MISSING`), unchanged. `PRESENTATION_WORLD_MISSING` remains only for a key that is not a world key (empty, or a path).

**Consequences.** `./mineworld-slice --world=bodies-yard` opens: its two rooms drawn from the server's walls, walked through their doorway by `--link`. Both shipped 3D packs carry `places/shells.yaml` and `far_field/bodies-yard.yaml`. 16f-c moves depictions into pack data; 16f-d moves the café's art into the default pack, where every asset collider is on `LAYER_ART` too. No server, protocol, kernel, contract or System changes (Q-16f-2 (B)).

**Limitation accepted.** The server discloses only the room the observer stands in, so a shell neighbour is drawn only once it has been stood in; until then it is a threshold. Where art and disclosure disagree, the body is no longer stopped by the art and may pass through a drawn wall; the geometry probe names the face. Glued frames overlap at a doorway by the two doorway insets (`ARC-45`), so a shell's walls can stand inside its neighbour's floor near the doorway. Offline, a plain world draws only the far field and the ground: the recorded observation the furnishings use exists for social-cafe only.

**Revisit** when disclosure widens to neighbouring places' shapes (Q-FD-3), so a neighbour's shell can be drawn before it is visited, or when 16f-d2 completes the move of the café's art into the pack.

## ARC-333 — Walkers yield by priority

**Date** 2026-10-20 · **Approved by** the operator at the S35 N-g design freeze (Q-Ng-1 … Q-Ng-6 as
recommended) · **Amends** `ARC-330` (R2's give-way also moves a walker of lower rank), `ARC-75`
(`Walking::started`, `WAITS_MAX`) · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-s35-ng-walkers.md`

**Problem.** After `ARC-330`, idle people no longer block doorways, but Market Town still ended
124–162 walks a month `stalled` (F-Ng-1). Two thirds of them fell in the 06:00 rush: most of the
apartments' residents start a walk out of its one door in the same minute, all aim at the door, and
stop one another within a metre of it. Nobody is idle, so `ARC-330` R2 moves nobody; the head-on bias
does not help walkers going the same way. Each stopped stride re-plans round the person who stopped it,
and after three strides without progress the walk ends. Separately, one planned stride on Market Town's
street was stopped by a solid at the same point again and again (F-Ng-4).

**Options considered.**

```text
WHCA* reservations (Silver 2005); Open-RMF's mapf     prevents meetings, but needs a new shared,
  crate (Apache-2.0)                                  time-indexed reservation table every step
                                                      reads and writes; stale on every re-plan or
                                                      push; mapf is f64 (breaks ARC-25 replay); still
                                                      deadlocks at a one-person door without priority
PIBT (Okumura et al., IJCAI 2019; basis of LaCAM)     adopted as a rule, not code: one step, local,
                                                      a total priority order, the lower gives way
fixed avoidance priorities (Unity, Detour crowd)      starve: the same person always waits
door queues (warehouse robotics)                      per-door queue points: more code, doors only
pass-through after a few blocked steps (RimWorld,     ruled out by the no-interpenetration rule
  The Sims)                                           (operator, 2026-10-19)
ORCA/RVO2, dodgy_2d, social force                     rejected in ARC-330 (common velocity tick,
                                                      floats); not revisited
```

No crate is added. The adopted rule extends code bodies already owns (`give_way` and the stride
resolver).

**Decision.**

1. **R1 — a walker of lower rank gives way to one of higher rank** (bodies). A walk ranks by
   `(started, walker id)`: the earlier `walk-started` instant ranks higher, the lower entity id breaks
   a tie. A person with no walk in progress ranks below every walker (`ARC-330` R2, unchanged). When a
   walker's stride falls short, `ARC-330`'s give-way may move every person in its corridor who ranks
   lower — idle people and walkers alike — with the same search, bounds and verification (V1–V4); a
   person who ranks higher is never moved by it. The walker who gave way keeps its walk and re-plans
   from where it stands. Facts are unchanged: presence's `arrived` with participants
   `[moved, mover]` and bodies' `gave-way { person, for }`.
2. **R2 — waiting behind a higher-ranked walker is not a stall** (movement). A stride stopped short by
   a person whose walk ranks higher counts as waiting: neither a stall (`STALLS_MAX`) nor a re-plan
   (`REPLANS_MAX`). `WAITS_MAX` = 20 consecutive waiting steps ends the walk `stalled`: about twenty
   strides of the walker ahead, enough for four or five people to clear a single-file door (a stride is
   1 340 mm, a doorway passage one to two strides a person), short enough that a true deadlock still
   ends within minutes. A pack constant, not world data.
3. **R3 — the stride and the planner agree on clearance** (bodies). The resolver's clear-corridor test
   (`Room::corridor_clear`) tested the axis-aligned box round the whole segment against each solid,
   while the route planner tests the segment against each grown solid exactly. A planned diagonal
   stride past a corner was therefore "not clear", went to the Rapier sweep, and the sweep stopped it a
   few millimetres short: a stop by a solid. `corridor_clear` now uses the planner's own exact segment
   test (`blocks`) against each solid grown by R + GAP — smaller than the planner's margin — so every
   routed stride takes the fast path.
4. **Contract.** Movement's public `Walking` gains the read-only `started() -> WorldTime`, stored when
   the walk begins, and a private `waits` counter; the `walking` component's schema version rises to 2.
   Bodies reads `started` by type, as it already reads `Walking`.

**Deviations from the frozen design, recorded at implementation.**
- PIBT's inheritance is realised flat: one stride's give-way moves every lower-ranked person in its
  corridor (at most `2 × NUDGED_MAX`), each to a point free of everyone; a moved person does not in
  turn push a third. The bound is the same, and a failed search moves nobody, as designed.
- AC-Ng-2's 1 m doorway already passed on N-f's head (nudges and the head-on bias); the scenario stays as
  a guard that only the lower-ranked walker ever steps aside farther than a nudge.
- Bounded discovery (Market Town, seed 1: R1–R3 as frozen left 157 stalls). Two more places let a
  lower-ranked walker hold up a higher one, and both are completed the same way: (a) a **nudge**
  (step-11 SD-B7) by a walker now moves only people who rank lower; a nudge that would move someone
  who ranks higher fails, and the stride is blocked at contact, so the lower walker waits behind
  instead of shoving the higher one back each step. A direct `move` still nudges anyone. (b) A walker
  **moved aside** by another person's arrival (presence names the mover as a participant) re-plans
  from where they stand, and that re-plan does not count toward `REPLANS_MAX`; when the mover ranks
  higher, the step also counts as waiting. Walking gains `moved_by` (schema 2). With them, seed 1
  ends 4 walks `stalled` in 30 days.
- Bounded discovery (crowded Commute Town, seed 7): `ARC-330` R2's final check used the fast path's
  test, which refuses a stride whose start lies within a radius and a gap of the floor's edge — a
  position a stride may legally leave. The give-way check now uses `Room::passable`: both ends inside
  the floor and the segment clear of every solid by a radius less the tolerance (the invariant every
  position keeps), and everyone else two radii and a gap from the segment. Pinned as a scenario.
- The scenarios live in `systems/bodies/tests/walkers.rs`, not `scenarios.rs`, which is past the size
  review threshold; a fourth scenario pins R2's queue (waits, walks on, stalls after `WAITS_MAX`).

**Consequences.** Every world with bodies and several walkers changes its facts, so a save from N-f is
not replayed byte for byte by this build (the `walking` schema is refused by name). Step-11's non-goal
"local avoidance of moving people" is narrowed: moving people now yield to each other by priority, but
there is still no velocity-based avoidance. F-Ng-2 (hands full) and F-Ng-3 (errand to an empty shop)
are not walking defects and were handed to N-h.

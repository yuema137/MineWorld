## ARC-290 — The far field is drawn in real depth order and judged by an ID render

**Date** 2026-10-18 · **Approved by** the operator (RL-e0b freeze, 2026-10-18: every question as recommended) · **Relates to** `ARC-23`, `ARC-102`, `ARC-103` (amended by addition), `DEP-45` (dated note covers ID frames) · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp1/pr-rl-e0b-far-field-polish.md` §§4, 6

**Problem.** Seen from 40 m, the far field showed five defects (RL-e0b D-1 … D-5): the sea covered Coronado, Point Loma stood too low, band and water edges ended in vertical cuts, the distant mesas were flat slabs, and a bare grass ring lay between the street and the distant town. The rendered check of `ARC-103` judges only presence below and above the horizon, so it could see none of them (RL-e0b A-7).

**Choice.** Five drawing rules: every camera-anchored column sits at a proxy depth that grows with its real distance, and the sea ribbon at the proxy of the sea horizon's distance; parallax with eye height is applied per column; band ends, the bay's sides and the sea ribbon's ends taper; near bands carry texture whose contrast fades with Koschmieder transmittance; the ring from the boundary is built on, with roofs capped below the sightlines of the protected eyes the scene names. Six criteria (FF-ORDER, FF-CREST, FF-SEAM, FF-RELIEF, FF-RING, FF-BAY), computed from flat-ID frames of the same run against the far-field file's own numbers.

**Not chosen.** Golden images (`ARC-103`'s reasons); per-defect hand checks only (a regression would merge unseen).

**Note, 2026-10-19 (implementation; operator ruling pending).** In a water sector a ring roof is capped below the water's far side (the foot of the nearest land in front of the sea horizon, or the sea horizon) instead of its near edge: with the near edge no box could stand within ~140 m of the street in the bay's bearings and FF-RING failed there. See the design's ledger, D-0b-1.

**Ruling, 2026-10-19 (the operator, relayed by the primary session).** D-0b-1: far-side rule accepted; near-edge alternative rejected. A ring roof in a water sector stays `CAP_MARGIN_DEG` below the water's far side, and the bay shows as a strip over the roofs.

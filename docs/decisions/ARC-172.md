## ARC-172 — Lakeside's sit points are fixed in S33-f and written into world data by S34-c

**Date** 2026-10-21 · **Status** accepted (operator ruling Q-S33f-1 (A), 2026-10-19, with S33-f's design
freeze; implemented in PR `mvp0/pr-s33f`) · **Relates to** `ARC-145`, `ARC-147`,
`.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-s33f-interiors-sit-points.md` §§3.2 (F-S33f-2), 5.2, 10 (R-S34c-f-1), 14,
`.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/step-34-posture.md` §14.2 (S34-c), Q-S34-9, Q-S33-8

**Problem.** Q-S34-9 ruled that S33-f "authors Lakeside's points once as world data". A place's
`sit-points:` section is refused unless `posture` is enabled in the world (`ARC-121` item 1), and enabling it
moves Lakeside's digests: a second recorded re-baseline, where Q-S33-8 allowed one (in S33-b). S34-c already
enables `posture` in every shipped world with its own recorded re-baseline.

**Choice.** S33-f fixes Lakeside's sit-point values once — keys, groups, `at`, `facing` — in its design's
§5.2 and lands them in presentation only: `presentation/mineworld-default/3D/sit_points/lakeside.yaml`, whose
`fallback` positions feed the shared sit rule's fallback branch (`ARC-145`). S34-c copies the table into
Lakeside's place files verbatim (`pier`, `square`, `bakery`; the halt's once S33-e2 draws its benches),
enables `posture` in `world.yaml` inside its own re-baseline, deletes the fallback positions from the
depiction file in the same PR, and runs the contested seat in the posture branch (R-S34c-f-1).

`tools/cli/tests/lakeside_sit_points.rs` holds the table to S34's checks now: written into a scratch copy of
Lakeside with `posture` enabled, the real loader refuses nothing; every point stands ≥ 260 mm from every
floor edge and solid; every drawn pelvis lies over its prop's solid; and the file's values equal §5.2's.

**Consequences.** Lakeside's world data and digests do not change in S33-f. The values exist in two places
until S34-c (the depiction file and the design's table), held equal by the test above (I-f-3); S34-c's own
check holds its world lines equal to the same table.

**Rejected.** (B) S33-f lands depictions only and waits for S34-c: Lakeside would show no sitting until
12d's chain. (C) S33-f writes the world data and enables `posture`: it splits S34's re-baseline in two.

## ARC-151 — The paced rule's generic offered band skips the action types the rule decides by name

**Date** 2026-10-18 · **Status** accepted (operator ruling "Fix it", 2026-10-18, in the primary session);
pending the operator's merge review · **Relates to** `ARC-34` (its 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-18 notes),
`ARC-27`, `ARC-35` point 6, `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-s27b-group-activity-offers.md` §15 D-b-4
(F-S27b-5), `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-fix-activity-double-draw.md`

**Problem.** Since S27-b, `group-activity` offers accept, decline, join and leave complete. The paced rule
controller already decides all four by name in its social band (`social.rs`: leave on 10 of 100 consults of
a member, join on 8 of 100 of a non-member). Its generic offered band (`offered.rs`: one available complete
affordance on 20 of 100 consults) then drew the same actions again, independently, on every consult the
social band declined. A member offered only leave complete left on 28 of 100 consults instead of 10.
Measured: activities lasted a mean of about 35–41 minutes (median 30 in `social-cafe`) instead of about an
hour, and 5–10 % ended within a minute.

**Choice.** `offered::attempt` takes the action types its caller decides by name and removes them from its
pool before it draws. The paced controller passes `DECIDED_BY_NAME`: `talk`, `walk-to`, `walk-step`,
`invite`, `accept-invitation`, `decline-invitation`, `join-group-activity`, `leave-group-activity`, each
spelled by its pack's own `ACTION_TYPE`. `offered.rs` still names no action type. The band's constants and
draw indices (14, 15) are unchanged. An observation is decided exactly as if the by-name affordances carried
no payload, which is the controller's decision before S27-b for every other action: no other action's share
changes against that input.

**Rejected.** (a) Draw over the full pool and discard a by-name pick: it keeps the doubled pool's dilution of
every other complete offer (a `buy` beside a `join` would keep half its pre-S27-b share). (b) Making the four
offers incomplete again: `ARC-34`/S27-b made them complete so that a requester that does not know the pack
(an MCP agent) can attempt them. (c) Retuning `LEAVES_ACTIVITY` or `ATTEMPTS_OFFERED`: treats a symptom and
moves every other complete offer.

**Consequences.** The long-run baselines of `social-cafe`, `market-town` and `lakeside` and the DL-a
30-day golden are re-recorded with a reason line; `bodies-yard` does not move. The `motivated` model has the
same double weighting (`options.rs`: `Choice::Leave`/`Join` beside `Choice::Offered`); it is recorded as
finding F-FAD-1 and not changed here. A future band that decides an action by name adds its type to
`DECIDED_BY_NAME`; the equivalence test in `offered_tests.rs` pins the rule.

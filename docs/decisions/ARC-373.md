## ARC-373 — `sleep-until { at }`: an exact, server-computed sleep target governed by `sleep`'s rules

**Date** 2026-11-27 · **Status** accepted (operator ruling Q-L2, 2026-11-27, "同意三个都按推荐冻结";
implemented in PR N2, branch `mvp0/pr-nightlife-n2`) · **Relates to** `ARC-283` (the sleep pack), `ARC-34`
(complete affordances), `ARC-151` (what a controller decides by name), `ARC-61` item 7 (configuration drift),
`ARC-63` (Interaction List sections), `ARC-370` (section schema versions) · **Design**
`.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-nightlife.md` §§0, 3 (N2-I1, N2-I1b, N2-I2, N2-I14), 6 (N2-C1)

**Problem.** A player going to bed at night wants to sleep until morning. `sleep { for }` asks for minutes,
so a client would have to compute "minutes until 07:00" from the clock — a world rule (what morning is, how
far ahead one may sleep) evaluated in a renderer, which `CLAUDE.md` §4 rule 15 forbids. The single-player
time advance (`ARC-374`) also needs a sleep whose end is exactly a known instant.

**Choice.**

1. **A second action of the sleep pack, `sleep-until { at }`**, whose alarm is `at` itself. It is `sleep` in
   every other respect (N2-I1): one admission in one order (`system.rs::may_sleep` — decode, living Person with
   no target, not asleep, located, bounds, place), the same Process, `fell-asleep`/`alarm-set`/`woke` facts,
   alarm privacy (holder-only `alarm-set` and `asleep.alarm`) and the same four ways to end. `Asleep` and the
   `sleep` Process are unchanged.
2. **`sleep`'s rules govern it; it adds no `ActionDecl`** (F8). Its place ruling and its parameters are resolved
   with `sleep`'s action type, exactly as `place.rs::allowed` and `interactions.rs::terms` already do, so every
   `{ action: sleep, … }` rule — place or actor form — permits or forbids `sleep-until` too, and no world writes a
   rule twice. A rule naming `sleep-until` is refused at load, as one naming `wake` is.
3. **Bounds in seconds.** `at − now` must lie within `shortest_minutes·60 … longest_minutes·60`; a past, present,
   too near, too far or overflowing `at` is refused `sleep-for-out-of-bound`, its detail naming the bounds and
   both instants. So the offer made at 23:00:12 for 07:00:00 is exact.
4. **The morning is a sleep parameter, `morning_minute`** (0 … 1439, default 420 = 07:00), regional like the
   others (N2-I2). The pack offers `sleep-until` **complete**: `at` is the first instant at that minute of the day
   strictly more than `shortest_minutes` ahead; the offer is available only when `at` is within `longest_minutes`
   and the place allows sleep, and otherwise refused exactly as dispatch would refuse it. A client copies the
   offer's request; it computes no target (`ARC-34`).
5. **A world rule in every world.** Shared and solo worlds offer it alike; in a shared world it is simply an exact
   alarm. Only the host's advance (`ARC-374`) is single-player.
6. **Hosted controllers never take it by chance** (N2-I1b, F1). The paced rule controller lists `sleep-until` in
   `DECIDED_BY_NAME` (its offered band leaves it out, `ARC-151`'s mechanism) and the motivated controller excludes
   it from `Choice::Offered`; both still decide bed by `sleep`. Without this a resident standing at home at 17:00
   would draw "sleep 14 h until 07:00" from the offered band (measured: 6 such requests in two days, seed 7).
7. **Versions and existing saves** (N2-I14, `ARC-370`'s precedent). The resolved section gained a field, so
   `SleepSystem` is version 2 and `sleep-interactions-configured` / `sleep-interactions` are schema 2. A Market Town
   save created before this decision is refused at resume by name — "the world's configuration differs from the
   save's … system 'sleep' … sleep-interactions-configured" — and its rows are left untouched; no migration.

**Reference value.** 07:00 is the operator's ruling (Q-L2). It agrees with the ATUS table the town's residents
already follow (`cognition/motivated/data/atus-a3-2025.tsv`, BLS American Time Use Survey Table A-3, 2025 annual
averages): the share of the population asleep falls through one half in the hour from 07:00 (659 ‰ in the 06:00
hour, 425 ‰ in the 07:00 hour).

**Consequences.** Removing the sleep pack removes `sleep`, `sleep-until` and `wake` and nothing else. Every
Market Town save from before this decision stops loading (accepted at MVP; a release note). Residents' behaviour
is unchanged: a two-day Market Town run (seed 7) prints the base build's summary line for line and every journal
row after genesis is byte-identical (`tools/cli/tests/sleep_until.rs`; evidence in the design ledger §12).

**Evidence.** `systems/sleep/tests/until.rs` (exact 07:00 target, bounds in seconds, past/now/overflow, place and
actor rules, regional morning, every way to end, alarm privacy, save → reopen → alarm),
`tools/cli/tests/sleep_until.rs` (rhythm regression against the base build's frozen summary; pre-N2 save refused
by name), `tools/cli/tests/sleep_sections.rs` (a rule naming `sleep-until`, a morning out of range, refused at load).

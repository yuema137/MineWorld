## ARC-281 — `ways`: the way to each place, as lengths and doorway counts, never durations

**Date** 2026-10-18 · **Status** accepted (operator, 2026-10-18: PR LE revision 2 frozen; Q-LE-3 as recommended) · **Approved by** the operator, in the primary session · **Relates to** `ARC-75` (route by the world, strides by the sender), `ARC-82`, `ARC-26` · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-le-leave-early.md` SD-LE-3, §0.3

**Problem.** To set out early enough, whoever drives a person must know roughly how long the way is. Movement deliberately knows distances and not durations: a walk takes no calendar time, and how long it takes is how often its sender asks for strides — every 30 world seconds in `mineworld run`, once a wall second (`time_scale` world seconds) hosted, whenever a human presses a key. A duration disclosed by the world would be a guess about the cadence, wrong for every host but one.

**Options considered.**

| Option | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| (a) **Its own record, `ways`, on the observer** | **Chosen**: the person's sense of the town, not a property of the room's doors |
| (b) Folded into the disclosed `passages` of the observer's place | Declined: describes another place's reachability on the room |
| (c) A disclosed duration | Declined: the cadence is the driver's (RC-3) |

**Choice.** Movement discloses, to a person about themself only, `ways: [{ place, doors, length }]`, computed at disclosure from presence's `Presence` and movement's `Passages`: breadth-first in `places_to`'s order (neighbours in `PlaceId` order), each reachable place's doorway count and the sum of straight segments from the observer to the first doorway, doorway to doorway through each place between, and 0 inside the destination (a walk to a place ends on entering it). A segment with an unmodelled end counts 0, so the length is a **lower bound**. Ascending by (doors, place), at most `DISCLOSED_WAYS = 32`; the observer's own place is not listed; nowhere to go is no record. `Ways` is a declared component type with an installed, never-written table, because perception discloses a record only when an enabled system owns its type (`owned_by_an_enabled_system`, the item catalogue's precedent). It is not a permission and not a route: every stride is still checked, and `walk-to` still plans through the wayfinders.

**Consequences.** No duration and no clock enter movement (I-LE-2). Declaring the type changes movement's declaration, so every world with movement — Lakeside and Bodies Yard included — has a different genesis composition row: `journal-300s` and `sealed-300s` move at that row only, while every summary and `facts-300s` stays (the FG-a precedent; `pr-le-leave-early.md` §7, Q-LE-3's accepted cost). Movement's version is not bumped. Each observation of a person in a joined world carries one more record (at most 32 entries of about 60 bytes).

**Revisit** when a travel system adds ways that are not walks (a bus, a train): they would be its own record with its own duration, since a timetable is the world's.

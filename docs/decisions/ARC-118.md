**Problem.** After `ARC-112` a buyer could only buy at the seller's ask or walk away. The operator's principle is
"any seller sets its own price, and bargaining exists". Bargaining spans time: the buyer offers now, the seller
answers later. Between the two, the buyer may spend the money and the seller may sell the item to someone else.
Something has to say what happens then, and who may see the offer.

**Choice.**

1. **State.** `Proposals { entries }` on the seller: a Person for its own asks, the operating Organization for a
   shop (a shop's entry records its place). Each entry is `{ id, buyer, item, price, place?, made_at,
   valid_until, status: open | declined }`, sorted by id. `id` is the `ActionId` of the `propose-price` request.
   `valid_until = made_at + 900 s` (`PROPOSAL_VALID_FOR`, 15 world minutes). An entry is **live** while
   `now < valid_until`. At most 8 live entries per seller (`PROPOSALS_PER_SELLER`) and at most one live entry per
   (buyer, seller, kind). `Proposals` holds no money and no item. Only economy's reductions of its own four facts
   write it.
2. **Actions.** `propose-price { item, price }`: with no target to the shop the buyer stands in (at the shop's
   place), with the person seller as target (same place, within `TRADE_RANGE`). `accept-proposal { proposal }`,
   targeted at the proposal's buyer: by the person seller (same place, within `TRADE_RANGE`), or by a keeper
   standing in the proposal's shop (same place as the buyer). `decline-proposal { proposal }` by the same people,
   no target, no spatial requirement beyond a keeper standing in the shop. `withdraw-proposal { proposal }` by the
   buyer, no target, no requirement. `price` is `1 … PRICE_MAX`; it may be above the ask.
3. **Judgement, in a fixed order.** `propose-price`: the buyer and seller as `buy` resolves them; an ask for the
   kind (`not-for-sale`); the price in range (`price-out-of-range`); no self-trade (`PreconditionFailed`); the
   limits (`proposal-limit`, detail `already-proposed` or `seller-full`); funds, stock, carry and the owners' own
   rules at the proposed price (`insufficient-funds`, `out-of-stock`, `cannot-carry`); then the requirement
   (`TooFarAway`). These checks at proposal time are an early, honest refusal; they reserve nothing.
   `accept-proposal` and `decline-proposal`: the actor a living Person (`NoSupportedInteraction`); the proposal
   open and live (`no-such-proposal`: unknown, answered, withdrawn, declined or expired); authority (the person
   seller itself, or a keeper standing in the proposal's shop: elsewhere `NoSupportedInteraction`, otherwise
   `PermissionDenied`); for accept, the target is the buyer (`PreconditionFailed`) and every `buy` check runs
   through the same function `buy` uses, at the proposed price; then the requirement (`TooFarAway`).
   `withdraw-proposal`: a living Person, the proposal open (`no-such-proposal`), the actor its buyer
   (`PermissionDenied`). Acceptance never reads the current ask: the seller chooses a different price on purpose.
4. **Settlement at acceptance.** One dispatch states `proposal-accepted { proposal, seller, buyer, item, price }`
   and then `ARC-112`'s sale: `sold { price }`, `money-transferred { price }` and `items-transferred`, through the
   same `settle` a `buy` uses. Economy reduces `proposal-accepted` into `Proposals` only, so nothing `admit_payment`
   or `admit_transfer` reads changes, and `ARC-112`'s atomicity argument holds unchanged.
5. **Facts and audiences.** `price-proposed { proposal, seller, buyer, item, price, place?, valid_until }`,
   `proposal-declined`, `proposal-withdrawn` and `proposal-accepted { proposal, seller, buyer, item, price }`,
   all `Participants`: the buyer, and the person seller or the keepers standing in the shop at that instant.
   Nobody else hears a proposal. A sale is still heard by its place. None is biographical. Each reduction first
   drops the seller's dead entries; a fact that breaks a limit or names an entry that is not open is refused by
   the owner (`FactRefusedByOwner`).
6. **Expiry is lazy.** No wake and no fact at `valid_until`; every judge, offer and disclosure compares the
   world's time with it. A declined entry stays visible to its buyer until then.
7. **Offers.** All four are incomplete. One `propose-price` per seller (refused only for reasons that hold for
   every kind: self-trade, `seller-full`); one `accept-proposal` per buyer with an open proposal the observer
   may answer, targeted at that buyer and available when one of them passes the acceptance checks (otherwise
   refused with the code of the buyer's lowest-id open proposal); one `decline-proposal`; one
   `withdraw-proposal` to a buyer with an open proposal.
8. **Disclosure.** The `proposals` record `{ incoming, outgoing }`, disclosed about the observer itself:
   `incoming` lists the live entries the observer may answer (its own, and a shop's while it stands in a shop it
   keeps), `outgoing` its own live proposals anywhere, each line with the seller's current ask.
9. **Version.** Economy's `SystemVersion` rises to 5; a save written before is refused by name (`ARC-25`).

**Consequences.** Market Town configures no haggling and no controller attempts an incomplete offer, so its facts
and summaries do not move. Its saves record economy's declaration, so `journal-300s` and `sealed-300s` move at the
genesis row only: a composition-only re-baseline, measured to move no fact or summary (Q-S31h-5, option (a);
Q-S31f-6 confirms it). An LM seat reads the offers and the record but attempts them only once the general
"attempt an incomplete offer by name" mechanism exists (Q-S31h-4). Agents reach the four actions through four
typed `act` kinds, not four tools (Q-S31h-3, `ARC-153`), added by S31-g or S31-h, whichever merges second. The NPC
options `haggle` and `accept_at_least_permille` belong to S31-d's `pricing:` block under the same rule
(Q-S31h-2).

**Accepted limitations.** One unit per proposal; the seller must be present (no remote haggling); a proposal
cannot be amended, only withdrawn and made again.

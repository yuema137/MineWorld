**Problem.** Only Organizations sold, at prices fixed at genesis on the shop's place (`ARC-38` item 3). A
Person could only give. A `buy { item }` named no price, so a price that changed between observation and dispatch
would be charged silently. Three different reasons a buy failed all read `TargetUnavailable` (step-10 QS-44),
because an offer could carry only one verdict.

**Options considered.**

**Choice.**

1. **Asks.** `Asks { asks: [{ item, price }] }` is economy state on the seller, a Person or an Organization, at
   most one ask per kind, sorted by kind, each price `1 … PRICE_MAX = 10^12` minor units. An ask covers every unit
   the seller holds; stock stays inventory's `Holdings`, read when shown. A seller holding none keeps its ask,
   shown out of stock.
2. **Shops.** `Shop { operator, keepers }` on the Place (component schema 2): prices leave it — the shop shows the
   operator's asks. Authored on the operator: `economy: { wallet, shop: { at, prices, keepers } }`; `prices`
   seeds the operator's asks. `keepers` names at most 8 Persons, each once. A Person's own asks are authored as
   `economy: { wallet, asks: { <item>: <price> } }`.
3. **Actions.** `list-for-sale { item, price }`, `set-price { item, price }` and `withdraw { item }`, each with
   an optional `for: shop` meaning "the shop of the place I stand in, for which I am a keeper". For oneself they
   require nothing of space; for a shop, being at the shop's place. `buy { item, price }` names the price the
   buyer observed: from a shop with no target, at the shop's place; from a Person with the Person as target, the
   same place within `TRADE_RANGE = 3 000 mm` (`give`'s reach). A payload without `price` is refused; there is no
   compatibility path (`CLAUDE.md` §4 rule 12).
4. **Named refusals** (`Rejection::System` codes, economy's own): `not-for-sale`, `price-changed`,
   `insufficient-funds`, `out-of-stock`, `cannot-carry`, `already-listed`, `not-held`, `price-unchanged`,
   `price-out-of-range`, `economy-currency-undeclared` (`ARC-111`), and the section's
   `economy-wallet-unresolved`, `economy-shop-on-person`, `economy-asks-on-organization`,
   `economy-reference-on-holder`, `economy-item-reference-only`, `economy-keeper-duplicate`,
   `economy-keepers-too-many`. Kernel reasons where they fit: `TooFarAway` (a Person seller out of reach or in
   another place), `PermissionDenied` (`for: shop` by a non-keeper), `PreconditionFailed` (buying from oneself or
   from a shop one keeps), `NoSupportedInteraction` (no such seller: no shop here and no target, or a target that
   is not a living Person with asks). This supersedes step-10 QS-44: `Offer::refused` carries the named code.
5. **Offers.** One complete `buy { item, price }` per ask, to a Person in the shop (no target) or perceiving a
   Person seller (targeted), available or refused with its code; the offer and `validate` call one function, and
   the owning pack's refusal comes before the spatial requirement in both (`ARC-34` note). `list-for-sale`,
   `set-price` and `withdraw` are offered **incomplete**, at most once each (Q-S31a-3): a generic controller
   attempts any available complete affordance at random, and a complete `withdraw` would empty NPC sellers'
   shelves. A complete offer must be safe for a controller to take at random.
6. **One settling owner.** Economy decides a sale in one dispatch and states, in this order, `sold { seller,
   buyer, item, count: 1, price, place }` (heard in the place), its own `money-transferred` (buyer → seller,
   through `pay`) and inventory's `items-transferred` (seller → buyer, through `transfer`). `validate` asks both
   owners' rules through their own functions (`admit_payment`, `admit_transfer`) before anything is written;
   the constructors ask again; each owner's reduction asks a third time, and neither reduction changes what the
   other reads. A refusal at reduction is therefore a defect in one of the two rules: the persisted world journals
   it as the request's fault with no facts (step-31 F-S31-8). The PR proves the agreement by a seeded property test
   and scans every Market Town save (I-S31-3).
7. **`ARC-38` item 5 is lifted.** Any kind may be sold; what a seller lists is content and the seller's choice.
   Inventory's capacity of six still bounds what a Person can buy (`cannot-carry`).

**Accepted limitations.** Buying needs the seller present or the shop's place; remote purchase with delivery is a
later pack on the same settlement. A shop with no keepers keeps its genesis prices. Haggling is `ARC-118`.

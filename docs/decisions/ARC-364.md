## ARC-364 — A shop sells only inside its authored daily hours

**Date** 2026-11-27 · **Status** accepted (operator ruling Q-L1, 2026-11-27, "同意三个都按推荐冻结";
implemented in PR N1, branch `mvp0/pr-nightlife-n1`) · **Relates to** `ARC-28`, `ARC-32`, `ARC-61` item 7,
`ARC-112`, `ARC-118`, `ARC-360` (`shop-stock`) · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-nightlife.md`
§§0, 3 (N1-I1 … N1-I8), 5

**Problem.** The operator-playable MVP needs a town with a night (`operator-playable-mvp-20261123.md` §1,
acceptance item 7): business availability is part of a night-to-morning routine. Economy's purchase
resolution had no time check, so a shop sold at 03:00 exactly as at noon. Lights, doors or a client-side
sign cannot implement it: a sale is economy's judgement, and a refusal must be the server's.

**Choice.**

1. **Hours are optional data on the `Shop`, owned and read by economy alone** (N1-I1). Authored on the
   operator's `economy: { shop: { …, hours } }`, carried by `shop-opened`, reduced into `Shop`. No stored
   open/closed flag, no Process, no scheduler: whether a shop sells is a pure function of its hours and
   the instant a read is taken at (`WorldRead::at`). The kernel, clients, controllers and other packs
   never compute business hours.
2. **Shape** (N1-I2). Absent ⇒ always open (every world before this decision). `[]` ⇒ closed all day.
   Otherwise 1 … 8 intervals `{ from: "HH:MM", until: "HH:MM" }`, half-open `[from, until)`; `until <
   from` wraps midnight; `from = until` is refused; two intervals may touch but not overlap once wrapped.
   Refused at decode, naming the interval's index; the loader names the file. Time of day is `at mod
   86 400` (instant 0 is local midnight, `INV-TW-4`).
3. **One judgement, `shop-closed`** (N1-I3). `sale::judge_open` runs first in `judge_exchange` and in
   `haggle::judge_proposal` (which does not run `judge_exchange`). Every way of buying from a shop agrees:
   `buy` offers and dispatch, settlement at resolution, the shop-directed `propose-price` offer and
   dispatch, the `accept-proposal` offer and accepted-proposal settlement. A proposal made while open is
   refused when accepted after closing. Precedence: not a living buyer / no shop / `not-for-sale` →
   `price-changed` (buy) or price range (propose) → **`shop-closed`** → self-trade → proposal limits →
   funds, stock, carry, owners' rules. A person selling for herself has no hours. Keepers keep listing and
   price authority while the shop is closed (N1-I5); sales are unattended.
4. **Disclosure** (N1-I6). `shop-stock` (`ARC-360` item 4) lists a closed shop with nothing stocked: its
   documented meaning becomes "asked, held **and on sale now**", schema 1 unchanged. A controller that
   reads it — the rule controller's fetch band — skips a closed shop without walking in, with no change
   to its code. The shop's `Listing` (prices in the window) is unchanged.
5. **Persistence** (N1-I4). Additive and optional, the `Shop.adjustments` precedent: `shop-opened` stays
   schema 2, `shop` schema 2, economy `SystemVersion` 5. A shop without hours encodes byte-identically to
   before, so worlds that write none keep their digests. Hours are re-validated on every decode.
6. **Existing saves** (N1-I8, `ARC-61` item 7 applied). Hours are section content, not a configuration
   fact (`configuration_facts` are `currency-declared` and `economy-configured`), so a save created before
   its pack wrote hours is not drift: it resumes and keeps its genesis terms — always open.
7. **`ARC-28` extended to a value type.** `ARC-28` lets a pack decode another pack's published *event*
   types with no system dependency. Economy now also reuses schedule's `TimeOfDay` *value* type, as
   employment already does (`ARC-32`: one time-of-day convention): a Cargo dependency only, no
   `depending_on`, so a world without schedule installs economy and keeps hours.

**Content.** Market Town authors the ruled hours: café 05:30–22:00, corner store 06:00–23:00, station
newsstand 06:00–18:00, each citing its evidence in the organization's file. Every resident routine visits
shops inside them (`pr-nightlife.md` §2.3). Social Café and Bodies Yard author none and are unchanged.

**Not in this decision.** Staffed hours (a keeper's presence), weekdays and holidays, door locks,
lights, private homes, sleep-until and its single-player advance (N2).

**Validation.** `systems/economy/tests/{hours,closed}.rs` (boundaries at `from`, `until` and across
midnight through every path; precedence; persistence and the pre-hours save); `tools/cli/tests/
shop_hours.rs` (a Market Town night and morning under `rule` and under `motivated`; the pre-hours Market
Town save). Evidence in `pr-nightlife.md` §§5, 11.

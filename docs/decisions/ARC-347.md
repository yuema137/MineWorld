## ARC-347 — Demo B is proved with the real clients, and the 3D client is removable by the same deletion check as the LM controller

**Date** 2026-10-20 · **Status** accepted (design frozen by the operator, 2026-10-20; implemented in
S14 PR 16e) · **Relates to** `ARC-23` (bounds decided before measuring), `ARC-47` (no rule in the
client), `ARC-174` (the parity test's file), `ARC-346` (Demo A, the 2D report), IC-7
(`scripts/ci_removability.py`), `docs/MVP.md` §§7.2, 9, 9.1,
`.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-16e-demo-b-proof.md`

**Problem.** `AC-13` for talk was proved between two flavours of a demo script, not between the real
2D client and the real 3D client; shove, kick and throw were never compared at all. `AC-15` was proved
with Rust connections standing in for the windows. Nothing checked that the two clients show the same
world, and nothing checked that the 3D client can be deleted without breaking the rest.

**Choice.**

1. **One step list, two players** (`tools/cli/tests/ac13_clients.rs`). The step list is 13b's selector
   format, built by the test from the seat's genesis observation (the nearest person offered `talk` or
   `shove`, the nearest loose object offered `kick` or `throw`, the first own-menu `buy` or `eat`), and
   played by the 2D harness and by the 3D market probe. Neither client names an action, a person or an
   object. Each request pair differs in `ActorLocation` at most (`differing_fields`), both answers have
   the same kind, and both saves record the same caused fact types. Worlds: social-cafe, market-town,
   lakeside (`eat`).
2. **The 3D client gains no menu for objects.** `{"open_object": id}` aims the reticle at the object
   (the ray must meet it) and opens your own menu (B); the choice is made among its entries about that
   object, which are exactly the offers the 2D object menu lists (target-less, the object in the
   payload). A talk entry takes typed words through `{"input"}`; with none it keeps its default words.
3. **`AC-15` with the real clients** (`ac15_clients.rs`): one hosted world with `--agent alice
   --save`, the 2D client and the 3D client seated at once; the four identity lines are checked against
   `GET /status`, the save's fact order and its head, and the story is read from the save (Alice's
   answer to the 3D window carries the 2D window's words).
4. **One world, two views** (`shown_parity.rs`, Q-PAR-1). The clock is paused through `/admin/clock`,
   and the two clients take the same seat in turn (`--hold 0`). Their `SHOWN` reports must be equal on
   `ADOPTION.md` §7's semantic keys. **Doorways are compared for the observer's own place:** each
   client learns a place's passages only where it stands, and the clients draw the ways back from
   other places differently (2D once per drawn anchor, plus the way back through a hub doorway; 3D
   every passage whose two sides are bound). A fresh seat has stood only in its own place, so that
   place's doorways are the common ground. A harness-only `--drop-doorway` on either client is told
   apart, naming the doorway and the client.
5. **Removal by deletion.** `scripts/ci_removability.py --target 3d` deletes `clients/3d-spike`,
   `mineworld-slice` and `mineworld-3d` in a scratch worktree of HEAD and runs `cargo fmt --check`,
   `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace` and the 2D client's
   scripted drive (`demo_a`). Any edit the copy would need is the check failing. `--target lm` (the
   default) is IC-7, unchanged.

**Rejected.** A new 3D scenario (`--scenario=ac13`): the market probe already plays 13b's steps
(Q16e-3). An object menu in the 3D client: that would be a new drawing for a harness need. Comparing
every doorway either client knows: the two clients learn and draw the ways back differently, so the
comparison would fail for no defect in either. A second removal script (Q16e-4).

**Consequence.** A new action joins `AC-13` by adding its selector to `Genesis::steps`. A world joins
by one test function. Known at implementation: several workspace tests that are not `#[ignore]`d read
files under `clients/3d-spike/` (the client-rule scans, the client-text scan, `furnishings.rs`,
`attribute_consumers.rs`). `--target 3d` therefore reports the 3D client as **not yet removable**
until those checks tolerate its absence. That follow-up is an operator question; it is recorded in
the PR design's ledger.

## ARC-170 — Lakeside's lane stands 1.0 m above full pool, the pier deck 0.70 m

**Date** 2026-10-21 · **Status** accepted (Q-S33d-1 ruled (A) by the operator, 2026-10-19) · **Relates to**
`ARC-177`, `DEP-77`, `DEP-78`, S33-a's far field (`presentation/mineworld-default/3D/far_field/lakeside.yaml`
`datum_m`) · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-s33d-terrain-water-exterior.md` §5.2, F-S33d-1

**Problem.** S33-a set Lakeside's lane 2 m above Lake Cuyamaca's full pool, approximate, leaving the grading to
S33-d. With a 2 m drop, an accessible fishing pier needs either a 16.8 m ramp at 1:12 (2010 ADA Standards
§405.2), most of a 20 m pier, or a deck at lane level 2 m over the water, which needs a 42 in guard (IBC
§1015.3) and loses the 34 in fishing rail of ADA §1005.2.1.

**Choice.** The lane's surface (scene y = 0) stands **1.0 m** above full pool; the water surface is y = −1.00.
S33-e's pier deck stands at y = −0.30, 0.70 m above full pool — below the 30 in (762 mm) drop at which IBC
§1015.2 requires a guard, so the 34 in fishing rail applies — reached from the lane by a 0.30 m ramp of 3.6 m at
1:12. The 3D far field's `datum_m` follows: 1 407 m (= 1 406 + 1.0). The 2D far field states no datum and is
unchanged. Every height is presentation; the server stays planar (step-33 F-S33-4).

**Consequences.** S33-e builds the deck and ramp to these heights (R-S33e-d-1). The values are approximate and
cited as such in `datum_source` and in the depiction file's terrain `source` fields.

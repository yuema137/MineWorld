## ARC-192 — A station is drawn from a station kit, placed at a site, bound by role tag

**Date** 2026-10-20 · **Status** accepted (design frozen by the operator 2026-10-19, Q-S29d-1 … Q-S29d-5 as
recommended; implemented in PR `mvp0/pr-s29d`) · **Relates to** `ARC-106`, `ARC-144`, `ARC-146`, `ARC-102`,
`ARC-103`, `ARC-307`, `DEP-8`, `DEP-16`, `DEP-60`, [`clients/3d-spike/PRESENTATION.md`](../../clients/3d-spike/PRESENTATION.md)
§6, [`MODULE_SPEC.md`](../MODULE_SPEC.md) §6.1 · **Design**
`.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-s29d-depot-exterior.md` §§5.3, 5.5, 5.7

**Problem.** The town's station is its landmark: the Santa Fe Depot of San Diego, at real size, at the west
end of the street (`ARC-106`). The operator ruled that the 3D building must be generic: built from data,
never hard-coded to one town, and every acceptance criterion must name a second world or a variant. Three
worlds already have a place keyed `station`, so the client may not even write that word as a literal
(`ARC-144`'s check (d)). And the exterior must be drawn from the first frame, before the server has
disclosed anything.

**Options considered.**

- (a) A GDScript building for Social Café. Declined: it hard-codes one town.
- (b) A building generated from the disclosed place shapes alone. Declined: nothing is disclosed before the
  first observation, and towers, arches and roofs are not in a place shape.
- (c) **A kit file in the Presentation Pack, a site per depiction, binding by tag set and doorway (chosen).**

**Choice.**

1. **The kit.** `presentation/<pack>/3D/stations/<kit>.yaml`, YAML's JSON subset (`DEP-16`), states one
   station building: its places (each with role tags, a scene volume name, a shell kind `main`, `annex` or
   `open`, and a floor rectangle), its doors (each joining the street — `from: outside` — or the main place
   to another place, through a named wall, with its leaves), and its number groups (`shell`, `arch`,
   `towers`, `arcade`, `annex`). Lengths are millimetres in the world's place frames (x east, y north), so a
   door position in the kit is the passage point a world states. Every place, door and group carries a
   `source`. The format is specified in `PRESENTATION.md` §7.
2. **The site.** A depiction says only where the kit stands: `{ kit, facade_x, axis_z, facing }`. Only
   `facing: east` is drawn, because a binding only translates (`SliceLayout`); a world whose station faces
   another way writes its frames for it.
3. **The builder.** `StationDepot.build` draws the main building, its towers, arch, arcade, annex and roofs
   from the kit at the site, registers its doors in the scene's door registry and makes one place volume
   per kit place. `StationViews` derives from the kit and the built depot the depiction's entries, perf
   views, landmark viewpoints and shots.
4. **The binding.** Each kit place becomes one depiction entry whose tags are the kit's (data, never a
   GDScript literal). `ARC-144`'s rule then binds whatever places the world discloses with those tags at
   those doorways; a place whose tags do not match is a reported miss, never a guess. A second world with
   the same station frames gets the same depot by placing the same kit; a world whose station is not its
   landmark does not match the depot's tags and needs its own kit.
5. **The refusals.** `StationKit` refuses, by name, before anything is drawn: `STATION_KIT_MISSING`,
   `STATION_KIT_MALFORMED`, `STATION_KIT_UNCITED`, `STATION_KIT_OUT_OF_RANGE`, and `STATION_SITE_FACING`.
   A scripted run exits 3 (RL-e0's pattern).
6. **Who reads it (Q-S29d-3).** Only the 3D client, at load. `mineworld packs validate` does not read
   kits: a second validator would be a second implementation of a file nothing else reads. Revisit if a
   second client reads kits.
7. **Batching keeps the station.** `SliceBatch` merges a primitive under a `station.` or `landmark.`
   category only with its own category, and the merged mesh carries it, so a check that hides the station
   by category still hides it after batching.

**Consequences.** S29-e adds a `fitout` group to the kit and draws the interior from it (benches and the
kiosk stay the world's furnishings, `ARC-307`). S33-e's Lakeside halt is a second kit or its own builder;
its role tags are data either way. The landmark check (`--slice-landmark`) takes the category prefix as a
parameter. The kit and the HABS references live under `presentation/`, outside the 3D export, so they add
nothing to `3d.pck`.

**Revisit** when a second client draws stations, or when a world needs a station facing a direction other
than east.

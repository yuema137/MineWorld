## ARC-360 — Putting things down, seeing shelves, keeping shifts

**Date** 2026-10-20 · **Status** accepted (operator freeze of `pr-s35-nh-hands-and-errands.md`,
2026-10-20, every question answered as recommended; implemented in PR `mvp0/pr-s35-nh`) · **Relates to**
`ARC-37` item 5 (amended), `ARC-26` (amended), `ARC-286` (amended), `ARC-280` / Q-LE-2 (amended),
`ARC-151`, [`MODULE_SPEC.md`](../MODULE_SPEC.md), `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-s35-nh-hands-and-errands.md`

**Problem.** Three milestone rows failed in Market Town for reasons that were not walking (N-g's trace,
#382 §2):

1. *Full hands (F-Ng-2).* A person carries at most six items (`ARC-37` item 5). People were given goods,
   and the fetch band bought kinds without knowing whether they nourish (coffee and tea bought as food,
   F-Nh-2). With six held, every `buy` is refused `cannot-carry`; the fetch band read only *available*
   buys, decided "here sells, nothing more", and did nothing, 33–108 times a month. Nothing let a good
   leave a person's hands except a `give` to somebody present with room.
2. *The empty shop (F-Ng-3).* A shop sells out by the afternoon and is restocked when a shift ends, at
   13:00 (employment states production in `ended`; this corrects N-g §2.4's "08:00"). A hungry early
   riser walked to the empty shop, which was also his punctual 08:00 part, and waited there 90 minutes:
   an absurdly early arrival (AC-LE-3). A memoryless controller cannot remember a shop was empty.
3. *The un-commuted shift (F-Nh-1).* The newsstand (S29-b) gave Market Town a third employee whose
   07:00 station part was not punctual, so the rule commute never planned for it and he arrived one walk
   late every day. `lateness.rs` and `punctuality.rs` also asserted literal employee and shift counts,
   which any change to the content breaks.

**Choice.** The world discloses facts; each controller decides by its own model; consequences stay with
their owners. Nothing names a place, person, item or world.

1. **A Place is a holder** (inventory; amends `ARC-26`). A living Place may hold items, unbounded like
   an Organization. A Place's `holdings` are disclosed to everyone who perceives the place (things lying
   in a room are seen); a Person's and an Organization's stay the holder's alone. Every `holdings` record
   carries `capacity` when the holder is bounded (a Person: `PERSON_CAPACITY`), so no controller
   hard-codes 6. The six-item bound is kept (Q-Nh-1); `ARC-37` item 5's reason is amended: a full person
   now has a way out.
2. **`put-down { item }` and `pick-up { item }`** (item-transfer). One item from me to the place I stand
   in, or from it to me; requirement: in that place; `pick-up` refused `cannot-carry` when I cannot take
   one more and `not-here` when the place holds none. Both state inventory's `items-transferred` through
   a new checked constructor, `transfer_in_place`, heard in the place. Offered complete: one `put-down`
   per held kind, one `pick-up` per kind the place holds. Items are conserved.
3. **What food would nourish me is disclosed** (needs; Q-Nh-3). `needs` gains `kinds`: how much one of
   every food kind of the world (every kind with a `nourishment` component) would nourish its holder, in
   `ItemId` order, beside `nourishment` (the carried kinds, unchanged in meaning).
4. **A shop's stock is disclosed** (economy; Q-Nh-4). A new view, `shop-stock`, lists every shop by its
   place and the asked kinds its operator holds at least one of (kinds, never counts) — none at a shop the
   observer keeps, where a keeper may not buy. Disclosed to each living Person about itself, only in a
   world that has a shop.
5. **The rule's fetch band reads reasons and frees a hand** (rule-controller `fetch.rs`; amends
   `ARC-286`). In order: pick up food lying here; buy the most nourishing kind offered available here (a
   kind that nourishes 0 is never bought as food); a nourishing pick-up or buy refused `cannot-carry` →
   free one hand by the policy's `free_hand` order (default `drink, eat, put-down, give`; Q-Nh-2; a world
   may reorder or empty it); waiting at a punctual place → nothing more; refused `insufficient-funds` →
   nothing more (F-Nh-3, recorded, no policy for being broke); with `shop-stock` disclosed, no shop
   anywhere stocking food for me → no walk; walk only to a shop-tagged doorway whose disclosed stock
   feeds me (or which `shop-stock` does not list); shop doorways none of which feeds me → no walk; a
   one-door place → out through it. A world without `kinds` or `shop-stock` decides as before. The rule
   never picks up anything but food, so goods put down stay put. `put-down` and `pick-up` are decided by
   name only (`ARC-151`): neither the rule's offered band nor the motivated model's generic option draws
   them by chance.
6. **A disclosed shift is an appointment** (rule-controller `commute::next_part`, the LM prompt's plan
   line; amends `ARC-280` / Q-LE-2; Q-Nh-5). Employment already discloses the employee's own job. The
   next part of the day becomes: the routine's next part at the workplace at or before the shift's start,
   made punctual (the earlier `from` at the place wins); else an earlier punctual routine part; else the
   shift itself (workplace, its next start, punctual). The motivated model reads the same function.
   Ivan's station part also gains `punctual: true` (content consistency), but AC-Nh-3 runs on a
   generated variant with every flag removed.
7. **Tests count what the run hired.** `lateness.rs` derives each world's employees from its `hired`
   facts and `punctuality.rs` its expected parts and shifts from `routine-assigned` and `hired`, never
   literals (F-Nh-1).

**Rejected.** *Discard* (destroys goods, hides a supply problem). *Sell surplus back* (needs every shop to
want goods). *Raising or removing the bound* (brings back step-10 F-39). *Weight or volume capacity*
(content for every item of every world, and still needs put-down when full; deferred, Q-Nh-6).
*Restocking by the hour* (supply timing is content; the early riser is hungry before any shift;
Q-Nh-7). *Controller memory of empty shops* (breaks the rule controller's "holds no world state" and its
rebinding guarantee). *Counting a fetch errand as an errand* (hides a hungry 90-minute wait). *A genesis
check for a job without a punctual part* (couples employment to schedule).

**Bounded deviations from the design.** (a) R3 named "every kind offered for sale where I stand"; the walk
rule of R5 also needs the nourishment of kinds stocked by shops one does not stand in, so `kinds` lists
every food kind of the world — a superset serving both. (b) R5 named "to anyone who perceives the shop's
place"; perception lists only the place one stands in, so a walker elsewhere could never see it, and the
record is disclosed to each Person about itself instead, as the currency record is (`ARC-111`). (c) A shop
the observer keeps lists nothing, since a keeper's buy is refused there; without it a shopkeeper would
walk back into his own shop for food he may not buy.

**Not decided here.** Rendering items lying in a place as placed objects in the clients (they appear in
the place's record first), and the no-interpenetration check that comes with that visual PR.

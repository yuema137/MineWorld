## DEP-144 — Runtime foot grounding: Godot's built-in `TwoBoneIK3D` and `LimitAngularVelocityModifier3D`

**Date** 2026-10-19 · **Status** proposed (DRAFT; awaiting the operator's freeze of step 36) · **Approved by** — (research plan approved 2026-10-19; takes effect at the step's freeze) · **Relates to** `DEP-20`, `DEP-34`, [`REUSE_POLICY.md`](../REUSE_POLICY.md) · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp1/step-36-interpenetration.md` §4.4 (PR S36-P4)

**Problem.** A retargeted walk rolls each body's own foot about its own ankle, so feet meet the floor at different
depths per body. S26-c-ground's `GroundContact` modifier lifts the hips so the lowest point stays on the floor. A
hips offset alone cannot put two feet on uneven ground (the lakeside's terrain, steps): one foot lands high, the other
low.

**Choice.** Use Godot's built-in IK, present since 4.6 on `SkeletonModifier3D` (our client runs 4.7): one
`TwoBoneIK3D` per leg (`UpperLeg` → `LowerLeg` → `Foot`), its target the floor hit under the ankle plus the body's
sole offset, and `LimitAngularVelocityModifier3D` after it to stop pops. The floor height comes from a ray against the
client's own colliders. Presentation only: it changes how a body is drawn and sends nothing to the server, which
remains the authority on where a person is (`DEP-20`).

**Why this and not another.** Built in, MIT, no addon to maintain; Godot's own 4.6 announcement uses `TwoBoneIK3D` for
foot planting. `SkeletonIK3D` is deprecated. The addons GodotIK (no release since 2025-06) and Many Bone IK (last
push 2025-09) are superseded by the built-in nodes. Navigation nodes are not used for this: routes are server-side
(`DEP-34`).

**Risk, accepted.** The 4.6/4.7 IK nodes are new; forum reports name errors in `CCDIK3D` and `FABRIK3D`, not in
`TwoBoneIK3D`. Only `TwoBoneIK3D` is used, wrapped in one script, so a fallback is one file. The asset gate G-4w
(PR S36-P2) stays in CI, so IK corrects millimetres, never centimetres.

**Consequences.** No dependency is added; the record exists because `REUSE_POLICY.md` asks for one when a built-in is
chosen over an addon or our own solver.

**Revisit** if `TwoBoneIK3D` shows a defect on our skeleton, or when a Godot upgrade changes the IK API.

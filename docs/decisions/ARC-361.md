## ARC-361 — Nobody trapped in a pocket or a doorway, and chance purchases within a budget

**Date** 2026-10-21 · **Status** accepted (milestone round 3, routed by the coordinator to the needs lane;
implemented in PR `fix/s35-nh-round3`) · **Relates to** `ARC-360`, `ARC-34`, `ARC-39` (entry, E3),
`ARC-75` (wayfinder), step-11 SD-N3 … SD-N5

**Problem.** Three milestone rows failed, and none was what its owner's test named.

1. *Sleep rows (AC-S35-8), Market Town seeds 1 and 7: 5.81 h and 6.89 h a night, 757 ‰ asleep at 03:00.*
   They predate `ARC-360` (the base before #429 measured 5.69 h and 4.78 h). People were not sleeping
   badly; they never got home. (a) An arrival at a crowded café door was placed by entry's E3 at the
   nearest free lattice point, between the door jamb and a potted plant: a pocket whose only gap (380 mm)
   is narrower than a person, so every later `walk-to` was refused `no-route` (3 098 refusals in 30 days)
   and the person stayed in the café for good. (b) Three shoppers at a shop door each wanted the doorway
   point and each kept the others off it; their walks stalled every consult, all night.
2. *Social Café (F-Nh-6), Alice 41 min late.* Walking out of the apartments at 05:00, she was stopped by
   one member of a group standing in the corridor after another: each replan avoided only the person
   who had just stopped her, and the walk stalled twice.
3. *CP-4, Bob's wallet below the cheapest price on day 227.* With the newsstand's clerk now working
   (`ARC-360` R6), eight papers a day are on sale; the rule's chance band (`ARC-34`) bought 328 of them for
   Bob (65 600 of his 200 000 endowment) and he gave them away, on top of eating every day. Before
   `ARC-360` the shelves were empty and his hands full, so neither drain happened.

**Choice.** Generic; nothing names a place, person or world.

1. **Entry places a person where a person can walk from** (bodies `entry.rs`, E3). The nearest free
   point is taken from the 4-connected component of the 50 mm lattice, over points where a person fits
   between the solids and clear of the objects, that holds the point nearest the arrival. A gap narrower
   than a person joins nothing. With no such point, the nearest free point at all, as before.
2. **A walker stopped short of a doorway and standing in it steps through** (movement `walk.rs`,
   `DOORWAY_REACH` = 500 mm, a body's width). Asked only after a stride to the doorway fell short, so an
   unhindered walk is unchanged.
3. **Stopped by somebody, plan round the people near me** (bodies `route.rs`): the replan avoids every
   other person within 3 m of the walker (`AROUND`), not only the one who stopped the walker; with no
   way round them all, it avoids the one, as before. Entry's reach test stops as soon as it reaches
   the candidate point, so neither change costs a search of the whole room on every step.
4. **Chance purchases within a budget** (rule `fetch.rs`, policy `needs.chance_spend_permille`, default 1):
   in a world where I eat (`needs` disclosed), a `buy` the chance band draws costs at most that per mille
   of my wallet. Food stays the fetch band's, by name. A world without `needs` is unchanged.

**Measured (local macOS, 30 days, before the machine was reserved).** Market Town seed 1: every seated
person asleep at 03:00 on 29 of 29 nights, 7.4–8.1 h a night; AC-S35-8 PASS on seeds 1, 7 and 42 in
Market Town and Commute Town; punctuality Market Town and Commute Town on time on seeds 1 and 7; Social
Café seed 1 on time (was one part 41 min late). CP-4, the guide and the remaining seeds run in CI.

**Rejected.** Moving the café's plant (world-specific). Retuning the offered band's frozen constants
(`ARC-35` point 6). Raising Bob's endowment (world-specific; it hides the drain).

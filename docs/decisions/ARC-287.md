## ARC-287 — Lateness and wage deductions: `late-for-shift`, patience as data, the optional deduction, a manager's `penalize`, `wage-deduction-due` answered by `economy`

**Date** 2026-10-19 · **Status** accepted (operator rulings R-LE2-1, R-LE2-3, 2026-10-18; Q-LE2-3, Q-LE2-4 as recommended) · **Approved by** the operator, in the primary session · **Relates to** `ARC-26`, `ARC-28` (a fact for another pack to answer), `ARC-29` (biography), `ARC-34` (complete affordances), `ARC-35` (no market crate outside `systems/`), `ARC-63` (the Interaction List), `ARC-282` · **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/pr-le-leave-early.md` §0.5, §0.6 (PR LE-b)

**Problem.** Arriving late for work had no consequence, so nothing in the world answered a person who never left in time. The operator ruled that lateness brings a complaint, and that a wage deduction matters but must be modular: a world switches it on or off and chooses how severe it is; where a shop has no boss the author's numbers decide, and where it has one the boss NPC decides.

**Choice.**

1. **`late-for-shift`, always on in a world with `employment`.** Stated at the first arrival at the workplace in a shift begun without the employee, when that instant is later than the shift's start plus the grace; or, at the shift's end, when the employee never came (`absent: true`, `late_by` = the shift's length). Payload `{ employee, employer, manager?, shift, late_by, count, level, absent }`: `count` is the latenesses of this job within the window, this one included; `level` is `notice`, `warning` (count ≥ `warning_at`) or `final` (count ≥ `final_at`). Audience: the employee and the employer's manager. Biographical. In MVP-0 `final` is a stronger complaint and nothing more.
2. **Patience is data**: employment gains its Interaction List section, `configure/employment.yaml` (`ARC-63`), flat parameters scoped by `actor` (the employee), `target` (the employer) or `place` (the workplace): `grace_minutes` 0 … 60 (0), `window_days` 1 … 60 (7), `warning_at` 1 … 20 (2), `final_at` 1 … 20 (3, read as at least `warning_at`), `deduction_enabled` (false), `per_late_minute` 0 … 10 000 (10), `cap_per_shift` 0 … 1 000 000 (600). A world with no section takes the defaults: complaints, and no money moves.
3. **A boss is a role a job describes**: `job: { …, manages: true }`, at most one per employer, a second refused at genesis by name (`second-manager`). A manager's own lateness is told to nobody else and never penalized.
4. **The deduction.** Off: nothing more. On, with no manager: employment states `wage-deduction-due { employee, employer, amount, shift }` at once, `amount = min(cap_per_shift, per_late_minute × ⌈late_by ÷ 60⌉)`, caused by the `late-for-shift`. On, with a manager: the lateness stays **open** on the employee's `Employment` for the window; the manager is offered one **complete** `penalize { employee, amount }` per open lateness, its amount the formula's (the bound), target-less and requiring nothing of space, so a boss docks pay from anywhere; the manager's own disclosed `employment` record carries `late_staff` (employee, shift, late_by, count, level, bound). Validation, in order: the actor is that employer's manager and not the employee (`PermissionDenied`); a lateness is open (`NoSupportedInteraction`); `1 ≤ amount ≤ bound` (`penalty-out-of-bounds`, naming the bound). An accepted `penalize` states `wage-deduction-due` caused by it, which closes the lateness.
5. **Only `economy` moves money** (I-LE2-2): it hears `wage-deduction-due` through employment's published type, as it hears `wage-due`, and moves the amount from the employee to the employer as one `money-transferred` caused by it, or records `deduction-unpaid` and moves nothing.
6. **Each controller decides as a boss in its own way.** `rule`: a policy block beside `commute`, `boss: { penalize_from: notice | warning | final | never (warning), share: 1 … 1 000 ‰ (1 000) }`; the first offered penalty whose level is at least `penalize_from` is submitted at `max(1, bound × share ÷ 1 000)`, with no draw. `motivated`: `Choice::Penalize`, scored `base.penalize` (600) × the `strictness` curve over the count (1 → 100, 2 → 500, 3 → 900), in the band draw at the offered amount. Both keep `penalize` out of their generic offered band, and both read the offer and `late_staff` by slug and JSON, never through the employment crate (`ARC-35` check 2). LM seats (PR LE-c) are told the complaint and offered the same `penalize`.

**Consequences.** Employment's and economy's `SystemVersion`s rise to 2 (their declarations grow), so saves of worlds with them are refused by name (`ARC-25`). A world without lateness keeps every byte of `Employment` (the lateness fields are serialized only when non-empty) and of `hired` (`manages` only when true). Worlds without employment are untouched.

**Accepted limitations.** `final_at < warning_at` is not refused at load (flat parameters have no cross-field bound in the SDK); it is read as `warning_at`. `Employment` keeps at most twenty lateness instants, the largest `final_at`, so a count saturates there. `penalize` names the employee in its payload rather than as the request's target, because perception asks for targeted offers only of the people an observer perceives.

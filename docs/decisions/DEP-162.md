## DEP-162 — Cast base colours ship at 1024² (Basis UASTC, RDO 1.0); amends Q-S26-5 and `DEP-160`

**Date** 2026-10-20 · **Status** accepted · **Approved by** operator ruling 2026-10-20 ("cast base-colour textures down
to 1024²; amends Q-S26-5"), relayed by the primary session · **Amends** Q-S26-5 (`.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/step-26-cast.md`
§10.3: "the 2048² texture stays the standard") and `DEP-160`'s "Why 2048² stays" · **Relates to** `DEP-161`, `ARC-80`
· **Design** `.structured-coding/plans/mvp0/step-23-release.md` §18 (MS-Ra-2)

**Problem.** After `DEP-160` and `DEP-161` the S23 packager measured `3d.pck` at 288.3 MB (hard limit 250) and the
macOS universal archive at 447.3 MB (hard limit 400). The 22 cast base colours (21 cast members and `meshy_d`, 2048²
Basis UASTC, RDO 4.0) were 74.6 MB of the pack. `DEP-161` listed the remaining levers; the operator chose this one, and
a shared Godot runtime for both clients (handled separately, because S23's probe P-3 found `--main-pack` unavailable in
official release templates).

**What changed in the ruling.** Q-S26-5 kept 2048² as the shipped standard. The ruling keeps 2048² as the *authored*
standard (the pipeline still bakes and stores `<id>_base_color.png` at 2048², PIPELINE.md §9) and ships it at 1024²:
`process/size_limit=1024` halves each side at import. A later ruling can raise the shipped size by changing one value in
`patch_imports.py`, without re-exporting a body.

**Options compared** (three cast members, Godot 4.7.2's importer; bytes per map as imported; PSNR/SSIM at the client's
closest framing — "face", a first-person eye 0.50 m from the face, two 0.25 m body radii touching, FOV 70°, 600×600 crop
— against the Lossless 2048² render; the 2048² row reproduces `DEP-160`'s numbers exactly):

| Setting | Bytes per map | face PSNR / SSIM (alba · dario · grace) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2048², RDO 4.0 (`DEP-160`) | 3.18 MB | 46.8 / 0.993 · 44.2 / 0.988 · 46.4 / 0.993 |
| 1024², RDO 4.0 | 0.88 MB | 41.5 / 0.979 · 39.3 / 0.968 · 40.7 / 0.982 |
| **1024², RDO 1.0** | **1.03 MB** | **43.2 / 0.986 · 40.8 / 0.977 · 41.9 / 0.987** |
| 1024², RDO 0 | 1.17 MB | 43.3 / 0.987 · 40.9 / 0.978 · 42.0 / 0.988 |

**Choice.** The `cast_albedo` role in `clients/3d-spike/tools/patch_imports.py`: `compress/mode=4`, `uastc_level=0`,
`rdo_quality_loss=1.0`, `process/size_limit=1024`, mipmaps on, `detect_3d/compress_to=0`. RDO 1.0 keeps almost all of
RDO 0's quality for 12% fewer bytes; RDO 4.0 at 1024² loses another 1.3–1.7 dB at the face for 15% fewer. At "talk"
(chest-up, 1.20 m) the chosen setting gives 53.3, 48.7 and 49.5 dB (SSIM ≥ 0.996), as good as 2048² at RDO 4. The face
close-up is visibly a little softer than 2048² (side-by-sides in
`mineworld-demos/video/verify-1019/fixes/cast1k-*-face-compare.png`); the ruling accepts that.

**Measured result** (S23 packager, `assemble --target macos-universal --clients 2d,3d`, the C6 tree with the `DEP-160`,
`DEP-161` and this record's imports): `3d.pck` **288.3 → 237.5 MB** compressed (the 22 maps 74.6 → 23.7 MB), under
its 250 MB hard limit; the archive **447.3 → 396.4 MB**, under its 400 MB hard limit by 3.6 MB. The packager's
`assemble` exits 0 for the first time (both still over their goals, 150 and 250 MB).

**Headroom.** 3.6 MB on the archive is thin: one more cast member adds about 3.6 MB (a 1.03 MB map and a 2.6 MB
scene). The shared Godot runtime the operator also approved would remove about 60 MB from the archive; it is a
separate change to S23's packaging and client start-up (P-3: official release templates refuse `--main-pack`).

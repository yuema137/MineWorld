# Character identity contract — the person in `04_character_closeup.png`

**Date** 2026-09-27 · **Source** `references/04_character_closeup.png` (1448 × 1086)
· **Governs** every 3D reconstruction of the MineWorld default character
· **Authority** `ARC-4` scopes what this reference is authoritative *for*; this document
states what it actually shows

Written by reading the image, at magnification, in four regions: head and hair, face,
torso and graphic, sleeve and strap. Nothing here is carried over from an earlier prose
description of the character, including descriptions written by reviewers of this work —
where a previous description and the pixels disagreed, the pixels won, and the
disagreement is noted.

This is a **contract, not inspiration.** The task it governs is the reconstruction of one
identified person. A candidate is measured against these facts individually, not against
an overall impression.

---

## 1. How to use this document

Each row below is a **checkable fact**. A review states, per category, the reference fact
and the candidate fact side by side and returns PASS or FAIL:

```
Outer garment FAIL: reference is an open burgundy cotton zip hoodie with a hood
  bunched at the neck, a cream drawstring and ribbed cuffs; candidate is a red
  quilted puffer jacket with a collar and no hood.
```

Comparative adjectives — "broadly", "roughly", "approximately", "close to",
"recognisably not" — carry no information and are not permitted in a fidelity review.
They are what allowed a categorical failure to be reported as polish.

### Hard-fail categories

Any one of these wrong means the candidate is rejected without further discussion, by the
agent producing it, before it reaches human review:

```
perceived gender presentation     outer-garment category
apparent age band                 a missing defining garment structure
body silhouette                   grossly wrong face shape
hairstyle category                missing identity-defining accessory
major hair colour
```

## 6. Overall identity — what makes this person recognisable

Stated so that a reconstruction can be judged as a whole rather than as a parts list. If a
viewer who has never seen this project is shown the reference and a candidate side by
side, these are the things that decide whether they say *that is the same character*:

1. **A young woman with a freckled face and a warm, slight, off-camera smile.** The
   freckles and the not-quite-at-you gaze do most of the work.
2. **A high, messy, warm-brown updo with loose strands** framing the face. The silhouette
   of the head is as distinctive as the face in it.
3. **An open burgundy hoodie over a cream slogan tee** — the specific layering, the hood
   behind the neck, the cream drawstrings against the burgundy.
4. **The mountain graphic and its two lines of text.** Legible or not at a distance, it is
   the one unique object on the character.
5. **The hand gripping the backpack strap.** It fixes the pose and reads at any distance.

A candidate that satisfies the table rows but not this section has been assembled rather
than reconstructed.

## 7. What this image cannot tell us

Recorded so that reconstruction does not silently invent, and so a reviewer knows which
differences are legitimate:

- **Back of the head, back of the hood, the bag itself.** Only edges are visible.
- **Below mid-thigh.** No footwear is visible in this plate. `01` and `05` show ordinary
  footwear on other townspeople; the character's own shoes are unconstrained here and any
  plausible casual shoe is acceptable.
- **Height and full-body proportion.** Cropped.
- **Hair colour under direct light.** The plate is a warm low sun; the true base colour is
  likely a little cooler than it appears.
- **Ears**, mostly hidden by hair.
- **Handedness, and whether the tee graphic is centred or offset** on the full garment.

Everything in §§2–5 is visible and must match. Everything in this section is plausible
reconstruction, and a reviewer may not fail a candidate for a choice made here — but a
candidate may not contradict a visible fact in order to satisfy one.
